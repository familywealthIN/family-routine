GoalsThe main Goals page, transformed: the cascade ladder is the navigation, there's a calendar of day rings, and every period is grouped by routine

What changed from GoalsTime.vue: the forest-photo hero with five "Total … Tasks" counters becomes a cascade ladder: Today → Week → Month → Year → Life. Each step shows a progress ring and its roll-up threshold (×5, ×3, ×6), and tapping a step switches the list below. The calendar's "N Goals" text becomes a ring per day. It shows only the selected week by default; pull it open for the full month and tap a day to load its checklist. Each period's goals are grouped by routine in time order. Year goals open the Year Goals page. The two FABs become a header "+" and a Milestones icon. Try: on Today, tick both Start Work tasks → "Ship the dashboard" reaches 5/5 days and auto-ticks → September's month goal goes to 2/3.
