Year GoalsYear goal page, redesigned in the Home language: ring + title, 12-month strip, the focused month's weeks and days, and a chat with the goal

What changed from YearGoalsTime.vue: the info alert becomes one rule line under the ring. The nested expansion panels become a month strip (tap a month) plus one focus card for that month. Weeks and their days are big checklist rows that expand in place. Edit/delete move to a ⋮ action sheet, and "Add month / week / day goal" are inline rows that open a create sheet with the parent locked. Thresholds come from autoCheckThreshold (day 5 → week, week 3 → month, month 6 → year) and status labels are Done / Active / Inactive. Try: in September, open "Ship the dashboard" and tick Sat → the week auto-ticks (5/5) → tick "Beta with 20 families" → September auto-ticks (3/3) → the year goes to 5/6 (83%). Chat: "plan the weeks", "add fix onboarding bugs", "how am I doing?". All frames share one state.

Getting to a year goal without the desktop dropdown: on phone, the Goals tab carries a live ring of the current year goal. Tap it, the title ▾, or the routine chip under the ring to open the goal sheet. It has search, plus "By routine time" (Now / Later today / Earlier today) or "By progress" sorting, and 62px rows, so it scales to one year goal per routine. You can also swipe the hero ring (or tap ‹ ›) to step between goals; the chip under the ring shows the linked routine and "3 of 7". On iPad mini, the Goals rail item opens a goal shelf that slides out of the rail, and the year band swipes too. On desktop, the sidebar lists every year goal (as YearGoalSidebar does today). Each goal keeps its own months, weeks, days and chat.
