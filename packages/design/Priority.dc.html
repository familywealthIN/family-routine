PriorityPriority page, transformed: triage the inbox into the matrix, the matrix is the navigation, and Delegate / Automate actually do something

What changed from PriorityTime.vue / PriorityMatrix: the four big count cards and the four stacked quadrant lists are merged into one 2×2 map. Each tile shows its open count and a done ring, and tapping a tile switches the list below. New items without a priority wait in a triage card: one at a time, sent to a quadrant with the 2×2 buttons. Lists are grouped by routine, as everywhere else. Delegate rows can be handed to an agent (it breathes while running), and Automate rows can be turned into a recurring routine task. Any row can be moved with the ⤧ picker. Try: triage "Fix flaky CI test" → Do; open Delegate → "Hand to agent" on "Summarize beta feedback".
