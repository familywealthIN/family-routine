GroupsGroups page, transformed: a live "today together" view of the people you share routines with, each member's week one tap away

What changed from FamilyRoutine.vue: the forest photo with a member count becomes a group pulse: this week's average and who's done today. Member rows keep the 7-day score dots from getGroupUserSevenDayScore, and add today's ring and the routine each person is in right now. Tapping a member opens their week grid (the FamilyUserHistory data: routines × days). On phone it opens in a sheet; on iPad mini and desktop it sits beside the list. Invite keeps the email form with validation and the 10-member cap; sent invites show as pending rows. Join requests show as a card with Accept / Decline. Leave group asks for confirmation in a sheet instead of a browser confirm(). Try: tap Priya; invite "jo@" (invalid), then "jo@family.com".
