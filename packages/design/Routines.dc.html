RoutinesRoutine settings, transformed: a 24-hour day dial, a timeline you edit in place, and an editor with steps, agent and year goal

What changed from SettingsTime.vue: the photo header and the Task / Time / Points / Action table become a day dial and a timeline. The dial shows each routine as an arc from its start to the next routine, with the current routine in orange and a NOW marker; the centre shows the selected routine or the daily total. Long stretches get an inline "+ Add routine" row. Tapping a routine opens the editor: name, time (10-minute steps, as in the form today), points, description, tags with your : levels (chips split by segment like GoalTagsInput; level-aware autocomplete: type area: to see its children, → or "n inside" goes one level deeper; Enter/Tab/comma/space adds, Backspace removes), reorderable steps, linked agent and year goal, delete. Saving re-sorts the timeline and redraws the dial. The edit/delete icons per row are gone; delete lives in the editor. Try: open Lunch Walk, move it to 13:00, set 10 points, add a step, save.
