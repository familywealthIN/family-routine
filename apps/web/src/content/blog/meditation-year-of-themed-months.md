A meditation practice can run for a whole year without going stale if the daily sit stays exactly the same and each month gets one new theme. In Routine Notes that means one fixed 06:20 routine plus a year goal whose month milestones each name a single practice.

## Why keep the sit itself boring?

Because a routine that needs a decision gets skipped. The sit is a container: short, early and identical every day.

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06:00 | Wake up | 5 | Open the curtains |
| 06:20 | Morning sit | 15 | Sit down and start the timer |
| 07:30 | Breakfast & get ready | 10 | Kettle on |
| 09:00 | Work | 40 | Close the inbox tab |
| 13:00 | Walk | 10 | Shoes on at the door |
| 18:30 | Cook & eat | 5 | Clear the counter |
| 21:30 | Evening sit | 10 | Phone in the drawer |
| 22:30 | Night routine | 5 | Lay out tomorrow's clothes |

The two sits hold 25 of the 100 points, which makes practice the second biggest commitment after work. The 06:20 window opens at 05:20 and closes at 06:50: room for a slow start, not for a lie-in.

The Morning sit checklist never changes:

1. Sit down and start the timer.
2. Three slow breaths.
3. This month's practice, which is on the routine card.
4. Two lines on the day item about how it went.
5. Cushion back in the corner.

Ticking inside the window is Discipline, and working the five steps is Kinetics. Neither one asks whether the session felt deep. The app scores turning up and following through, and the practitioner judges the rest.

## Where does the variety come from?

From the cascade. The year goal reads "A year of daily practice, one theme a month", and every month milestone names a practice:

| Month | Theme | Week milestone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| January | Counting the breath | Five mornings counting cycles of ten |
| February | Body scan | Five mornings, feet to crown |
| March | Sounds and open awareness | Five mornings resting with sounds |
| April | Loving-kindness phrases | Five mornings of four phrases |
| May | Walking practice | Three walking sits, two seated |
| June | Noting thoughts | Five mornings noting "planning" |

![A year goal with its ring, the 12-month strip across the top and the focused month's weeks and day items below](/screenshots/guide/goals-cascade.jpg)

On the Year Goals page, the 12-month strip shows which themes are met and which are still open ([the Goals chapter](/guide/goals) walks through it). Each week milestone splits into day items linked to the Morning sit, for example "Body scan, 15 minutes". Because the item is linked to the routine, it appears on the routine's card under the Today, Week, Month and Year tabs, so February's theme is already waiting on the cushion.

Linked items are where Geniuses points come from, and an item that sits under a week, a month and the year outweighs a loose one. Editing the plan counts as well. If a full body scan doesn't fit in fifteen minutes, rename the February milestone "Short scan, feet to knees" in the middle of the month. Shaping the plan to the practice is exactly what the G pillar rewards.

## How does February actually close?

Here's the arithmetic for one month:

- A day counts as met when every item linked to that week on that date is ticked.
- A week closes at 5 met days, so 6 sits in a week leave one spare morning.
- The month closes at 3 met weeks. Four weeks were planned, so one rough week is absorbed.
- The year closes at 6 met months. Planning twelve themes is ambition, not a higher bar.

A practitioner who loses most of August to travel still finishes the year on schedule. When you plan the second half, read your two-line notes first. July's best theme is often a spring month you rushed through.

## What happens when the sit runs late?

A phone call pushes the sit to 07:05, past the 06:50 close. On Home the routine moves to **Past**. That same day, spend points to check it in late: the streak survives, and the check-in earns its points like an on-time one. The day item ticked at 07:05 reads Missed, since status records timing, but it still counts toward the week.

If late sits become a habit, fix the schedule rather than your willpower. Open More → Routines, move the start to 06:40, or switch the reminder from Notify to Alarm so it rings at 06:20 and again five minutes later. For genuine sick or travel days, long-press today in the week strip and take a Skip Day. You get two a week at most, and they don't break the streak.

## Starting next month

- Write the year goal, then only the next month's theme.
- Link every day item to the Morning sit so the theme shows on the card.
- Keep notes to two lines a sit.
- At month end, check the Goals calendar for blank weeks before they turn into a blank month.
