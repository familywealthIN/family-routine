A teacher can plan the year in half-terms: term-time routines built around the timetable, two month goals for each half-term, and a lighter plan for the holidays. A school year doesn't run January to December. It runs in blocks of six or seven weeks flat out, each followed by a week off. A system that treats every week the same will be wrong for a third of the year.

## The term-time set

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06:10 | Wake up | 5 | Kettle on |
| 06:40 | Lesson check | 10 | Open today's timetable |
| 07:30 | Commute | 5 | Bag packed the night before |
| 08:20 | Teaching day | 30 | Register open |
| 16:00 | Marking | 20 | One stack on the table |
| 17:30 | Exercise | 10 | Shoes on |
| 19:00 | Family evening | 10 | Laptop in the bag |
| 21:30 | Plan tomorrow | 10 | Tomorrow's first lesson open |

Teaching day carries 30 of the 100 points, but it isn't where the plan gets better. That happens in the 20-point Marking routine.

## Why make marking a routine?

Because marking expands to fill whatever evening it's given. As a routine it has a start time, 16:00, a window from 15:00 to 16:30, and a first step that barely counts as a task: one stack on the table. The rest of the checklist is short: forty minutes on a timer, three whole-class feedback points, then the stack goes back in the bag.

Working through the steps earns Kinetics, and ticking inside the window earns Discipline. Starting straight after the last lesson, before the drive home, means the marking gets done at school and the evening stays free.

## The year goal it feeds

The year goal is to return every piece of marked work within a week. It's specific, and students notice. Year goals follow the calendar, so this one runs through the spring and summer terms and into the autumn. The habit carries across the summer break even though the timetable doesn't.

![A year goal with its ring, the 12-month strip and the focused month's weeks and days](/screenshots/guide/goals-cascade.jpg)

The Year Goals page shows all twelve months on one strip ([the Goals chapter](/guide/goals) walks through it). Each half-term spans about two calendar months, so it gets two month milestones. For example, September is baseline assessments marked and returned within five school days, and October is coursework drafts back with written feedback before the break. Each week milestone is a marking stack cleared on five days. Each day item names a real stack, such as "Year 10 essays, set 2", and is linked to Marking so it appears on the card. Every ticked stack earns Geniuses.

The thresholds suit a school calendar. Five teaching days can meet a week. A month needs three good weeks, so an exam week or a training day doesn't sink it. The year needs six good months, and term time takes up most of the calendar.

## When a school day slips

Parents' evening runs until 20:00, and the Marking window closed at 16:30. If a stack still got marked at 21:00, spend 20 points from **Past** to check the routine in that evening. It's the same day, the streak holds, and the tick counts in full. If nothing got marked, leave it. The week has four other teaching days, and the weekend if you want it.

A stack marked on Tuesday but only ticked on Thursday still counts toward the week. It will just read Missed, because the status records when the tick happened.

## The half-term week

A week off isn't something Skip Day is meant to cover. You get two Skip Days a week, and they're for rest days, not whole holidays. Change the plan for the break instead, under More → Routines:

- Rename Teaching day to "Holiday project" and move it to 09:30.
- Turn Marking into reading for next half-term's topics.
- Keep Wake up, Exercise and Family evening at the same points, just later in the day.

Then use Skip Day on the two days you really want off. Changing the plan to fit the season isn't cheating. Improving the plan is exactly what Geniuses rewards.

## The long summer

In the last week of July, spend one Plan tomorrow session on the AI plan. Open the add box, choose the Goal tab, and type "Return marked work within a week, every half-term next year". The AI drafts the month milestones. Edit them to match the real term dates before September arrives.

## Your next step

Add Marking as its own 20-point routine, starting the minute your last lesson ends. Then add this week's actual stacks as day items.
