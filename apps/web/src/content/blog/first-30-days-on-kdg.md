Your first 30 days on the KDG framework go best set up week by week: Discipline first, then Kinetics, then Geniuses. A common mistake is setting everything up on day one, with twelve routines, four year goals and an agent on every slot. It looks great that first evening. By day nine half of it is in **Past**, and the app feels like a list of things you've let slip.

The framework runs as a loop: **show up on time → act with full dedication → improve the plan**. Setting it up in the same order works too.

## Days 1–7: five routines and nothing else

Start with five routines at times you already keep. Not the times you'd like to keep. The ones you keep now.

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06:30 | Wake up | 15 | Glass of water |
| 07:00 | Move | 20 | Shoes on |
| 09:00 | Work block | 35 | Today's first task open |
| 18:30 | Learn | 15 | Book or course open |
| 22:00 | Night | 15 | Phone charging outside the bedroom |

That's the full 100-point daily budget. No single routine can go above 50, and the editor shows what's left as you allocate. Give the most to the routine that matters most, which for most people is the work block.

Week one has one target: **tick each routine inside its window**. Each window is 1h 30m, from an hour before the start to half an hour after, so Move at 07:00 counts anywhere from 06:00 to 07:30. On-time ticks earn **Discipline**.

Open **More → Notifications** and switch on Good morning, set a little after you usually wake. It shows the day at a glance, starting with what's first.

## Days 8–14: steps that pull you in

Once the routines are happening, make them do something. New routines come with suggested standard steps. Edit them until each checklist reads like instructions:

- **The first step is small and physical.** "Shoes on", "Mat unrolled", "Laptop open". The reminder opens with it, so make it the easiest thing on the list.
- **Three to six items.** More than that and you'll stop ticking them.
- **Verbs, not labels.** "Run the river loop", not "Cardio".

![Home on a phone: the week strip of day rings, a routine in progress with its checklist and Add task, and the Home, Priority, Agents and Goals bar](/screenshots/home-mobile.png)

The checklist sits on the routine's card on Home, with **Add task** underneath for anything unplanned ([the Dashboard chapter](/guide/dashboard) tours the screen). Items ticked there, plus quick tasks finished while the routine runs, earn **Kinetics**: the difference between turning up and doing the work.

## Days 15–21: your first cascade

Now add one year goal, just one, and link it to one routine. Say it's "Read 20 books this year", tied to Learn:

- **Year:** Read 20 books this year.
- **Month:** Two books in March.
- **Week:** Read on five days.
- **Day:** "Chapters 3–5", linked to Learn.

The day item appears on the Learn card, and the card's Week, Month and Year tabs show where it sits. Ticks on linked items earn **Geniuses**, and each step up the plan is worth more. Five good days complete a week, three good weeks a month, and six good months the year. If you're stuck on how to split a goal, tap the **+** beside the chat box, open the Goal tab, type the goal as a prompt, and edit what the AI plan suggests.

## Days 22–30: what if you miss something?

By now you will have, and that's expected. There are three tools for it:

1. **A late check-in.** When a window closes, the routine moves to Past. Later that day you can spend points to check it in. The streak holds, any agents on the routine still run, and the tick earns its points like any other. Onboarding gives you 300 welcome points, and each day's earnings are added overnight.
2. **Skip Day.** Long-press today in the week strip for a rest day. It costs no streak, and you get two a week.
3. **Late goal ticks.** A day item you forgot can be ticked tomorrow and still counts toward its week. It will show **Missed**, because the label records timing, but the week still moves.

## Day 30: the first review

Open **More → Progress** and switch to Month. *On the clock* shows which routines you're always late for. The D, K and G totals show your weakest pillar. Then change one thing: a time, a step, or a second year goal. Only one.

After that, the setup is over. The month you've just finished is the method: show up, do the work, improve the plan, then do it again.
