Job hunting works as a week goal: five tailored applications, mock interviews in the afternoons, and the five-day rule deciding when the week counts. Without that structure, looking for work is a full-time job with no manager, no feedback and a lot of silence. One rejection email can wipe out an afternoon, and by Thursday it's hard to say whether the week was productive or just busy.

The way out is to measure the effort, which you control, rather than the outcome, which you don't. Here's an illustrative setup in Routine Notes.

## Treat the search as a working day

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 07:00 | Wake & move | 10 | Trainers on |
| 08:00 | Search & shortlist | 15 | Open the three job boards |
| 09:00 | Applications block | 30 | Open the tailored CV template |
| 12:30 | Walk | 5 | Jacket on, phone stays home |
| 14:00 | Interview practice | 20 | Open today's question set |
| 16:00 | Skill course | 10 | Course page open |
| 21:30 | Wind-down | 10 | Laptop closed, lid down |

That's 100. The applications block gets 30 because tailored applications are where most jobs are won or lost, and they're the first thing to slide into "I'll just browse a bit more". Interview practice gets 20 because it's the one everyone skips.

When nobody expects you anywhere, the window is the clock that gets you to the desk. The 09:00 block counts as on time from 08:00 to 09:30, and ticking inside that earns **Discipline**.

## Steps that stop the drift

The applications block has five steps:

1. Open the tailored CV template.
2. Pick one role from this morning's shortlist.
3. Rewrite the summary and three bullets for it.
4. Write a four-sentence cover note.
5. Submit and log it.

Interview practice has its own: pick a question, answer it out loud, record it, play it back, write one improvement. Ticked items earn **Kinetics**. One application done properly is worth more than twenty skimmed.

## The cascade, from a year to a Tuesday

- **Year:** "Land a role I'd be glad to stay in for three years."
- **Month:** "Twenty tailored applications, eight mock interviews."
- **Week:** "Five tailored applications."
- **Day:** "Apply: operations analyst role", linked to Applications block.

Ticking a linked day item earns **Geniuses**, and the bigger horizons weigh more. A month completes after three good weeks. A year needs six good months, which leaves room for the goal to carry on into the new job: "Pass probation" and "Lead one improvement" can be its later milestones.

## How does the five-day rule work?

A week goal completes once **five of its days are met**. A day is met when every item tied to the week on that date is ticked. Two things follow from that.

**Spread the work.** Five applications all sent on Monday is one met day, not five. Put one application on each weekday and every day can count.

**Keep each day finishable.** If Tuesday has three week-goal items and you finish two, Tuesday isn't met. Plan one solid item per day for the week goal. If you have energy left, add more work as quick tasks in the block rather than as extra week-goal items.

## Rejection days and late starts

Some mornings a rejection lands at 09:05 and the applications block doesn't start until 11:00. By then the window has closed and the routine is in **Past** on Home. If the application went out, check it in from Past before the day ends, using points. The streak holds and the tick earns its points in full. Onboarding gives you 300 welcome points, and each day's earnings are added overnight.

If you sent an application and forgot to tick it, tick it tomorrow. It will show **Missed**, because that label is about timing, but the week still counts it.

If you need a day off, take it on purpose: long-press today in the week strip and pick **Skip Day**. You get two a week, they go down as rest, and the streak stays. A day off you chose is better than one spent feeling guilty all afternoon.

## Friday's ten minutes

Open **More → Progress** on the Week view. Check whether the week goal completed, read *On the clock* to see which routines started late, and look at the D, K and G totals. Then set next week's five day items. Once interviews start coming in, shift points from searching to practice in **More → Routines**. The plan should change as the search moves on.
