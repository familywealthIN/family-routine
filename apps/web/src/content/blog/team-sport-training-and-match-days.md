A footballer's routine has three kinds of evening: training nights, match days and the nights in between, and one routine at 19:30 can hold all three. In a team sport most of the schedule belongs to someone else. Training is Tuesday and Thursday because that's when the pitch is booked, and kick-off is Saturday at 14:00 because the league says so. What you control is turning up on time and ready, and what you do on the other nights.

## One evening routine, three kinds of night

Routines repeat every day, so there's no "Tuesday training" routine sitting empty the rest of the week. There's one Football routine, and the day items decide what it holds tonight.

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 (the cue) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06:40 | Wake up | 5 | Glass of water |
| 07:00 | Breakfast & commute | 10 | Bag by the door |
| 08:30 | Work | 40 | Open today's list |
| 12:30 | Lunch walk | 5 | Stand up from the desk |
| 19:30 | Football | 25 | Boots and shin pads into the bag |
| 21:30 | Stretch & review | 10 | Mat unrolled |
| 22:30 | Night routine | 5 | Phone on charge in the hall |

The day adds up to 100. Football's window opens at 18:30 and closes at 20:00, which covers the drive and the squad warm-up. Getting there on time is what the routine is for, and ticking it as you walk out to the pitch is Discipline at its plainest.

**Training nights (Tuesday, Thursday).** The routine is the session itself: boots into the bag, arrive and warm up with the squad, the session, a cool-down lap.

**Off nights (Monday, Wednesday, Friday).** Same routine, same time, but at home: twenty minutes of passing against a wall, or a run. It's short on purpose. All it needs to do is keep 19:30 meaning football.

**Match day (Saturday).** Kick-off at 14:00 is four and a half hours before the routine's window opens. The match goes in as a day item linked to Football, and the 19:30 slot becomes recovery: a stretch, a walk, a note on how the game went.

## Where does Kinetics come from?

Mostly from the 21:30 Stretch & review, which has four steps: unroll the mat, ten minutes of stretching, note one thing from tonight, check tomorrow's item. Each tick in the slot earns Kinetics. Over a season, those notes pile up into a few hundred small lessons. When something comes up mid-session that you want to remember, add it with the **+** as a quick task.

The add box drops the note straight into tonight's Football slot, so it doesn't end up somewhere you'll never look again ([the Dashboard chapter](/guide/dashboard)).

## What goal fits a whole season?

Pick one about your own game, not the league table:

- **Year:** Play confidently with my weaker foot
- **Month:** "Weaker foot: passing over 10 metres"
- **Week:** "Two training sessions, three home sessions"
- **Day:** "Wall passes, weaker foot, 200 touches"

Two training nights plus three home nights make five met dates, which is what closes the week. Three closed weeks complete a month, and six completed months close the season's goal. The league table never comes into it.

## When the late night or the away weekend breaks the week

- **Training ran over.** You tick at 20:15 and Football has already moved to **Past** on Home. Check it in from there before midnight for its 25 points, and the streak carries on. Your on-time Tuesdays, which settle overnight, pay for the occasional late Thursday.
- **Cup weekend away.** Long-press today in the week strip for a Skip Day. You get two a week, which covers Saturday and Sunday exactly, and the record shows rest.
- **You just didn't go.** Leave it unticked. A season with honest gaps tells you more than one that looks spotless.

If you're late most Thursdays, change the plan, not the effort. Move the routine to 19:40 in **More → Routines**, or tick it as you arrive, before the warm-up. After a month, **More → Progress → On the clock** will show which fix you needed.

## This week

Set up the single Football routine and write three different day items: one training night, one home night, and Saturday's match. If 19:30 shows the right kind of evening every day without you having to think about it, the setup works.
