A kitchen iPad keeps the morning routine on the counter, where a glance from the kettle shows what comes next. Turn on Always on display, sign it in to whoever runs the mornings, and give the routines names you can read from across the room. A phone goes back in a pocket and takes the routine with it. A screen propped on the counter stays where the morning happens.

## 1. Whose routines does the counter screen show?

The iPad signs in to one account, so it shows that person's routines. In most homes that's whoever runs the mornings. Everyone else keeps their own routines on their own phones.

Say this out loud at home: the counter screen is one person's checklist, and anyone walking past can read it.

## 2. Turn on Always on display

Go to **More → Profile → Display**. The card only appears on an iPad or tablet. Set **Always on display** to On and the screen won't sleep while Routine Notes is open. Leave the iPad on charge, because it will be awake all morning. Close the app and the screen sleeps as normal, so it isn't lit all day unless you leave it open.

## 3. Write routines you can read from two metres away

From across a room you need short names and short lists. "Lunches packed" works. "Morning logistics block" doesn't.

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06:30 | Kitchen open | 10 | Lights on, kettle on |
| 07:00 | Breakfast | 10 | Bowls on the table |
| 07:30 | Lunches packed | 10 | Boxes on the counter |
| 07:50 | Out of the door | 15 | Bags by the door |
| 09:00 | Work | 35 | Notebook open |
| 18:00 | Dinner | 10 | Clear the counter |
| 21:00 | Kitchen close | 10 | Wipe the counters |

The seven routines use the full 100-point budget. Four of them, worth 45 points between them, happen at the counter itself. That's why the screen lives there.

## 4. What does everyone see?

![Home with the week strip, the focused routine card and its checklist, the view the counter screen shows](/screenshots/guide/dashboard-day.jpg)

The week strip runs along the top, with today's dial filling as routines are ticked. Below it, the focused card shows whatever is due now with its checklist, and **Upcoming** lists what follows ([the Dashboard chapter](/guide/dashboard) labels each part). When a child asks "what now?", point at the screen: Breakfast is done, Lunches is up, Out of the door is next.

The card follows the clock, so evening routines appear at their times too. If any of yours are private, the easiest fix is to close the app once the morning is over.

## 5. Ticking from the counter

Each window opens sixty minutes before a routine's start and shuts thirty minutes after, so with routines this close together several are open at once. Lunches at 07:25 and Out of the door at 07:45 both count as on time.

A card you can see helps **Discipline** in the simplest way: it's hard to forget a routine that's in front of you while you pour the milk. Each step ticked as the morning goes on earns **Kinetics**.

## What happens when the 07:50 routine is missed?

Sometimes Out of the door is still unticked at 08:30 because everyone left in a rush. It has dropped into **Past**, and the counter screen will show it there when you get home. If you did leave, just late, open it and spend points to check it in. The streak continues and the tick counts fully. The points come from earlier days, since each day's earnings settle overnight.

On a morning with no school run at all, long-press today in the week strip and take **Skip Day**. You get two a week, and they don't break the run.

## A goal for the household morning

Link the routine that matters most, Out of the door, to one goal. The week milestone is "Out of the door by 07:50 on five days", under a term-long month milestone and a year goal of calmer school-day mornings. Five good mornings finish the week, three good weeks finish the month, and six good months finish the year. A morning ticked late still adds to the count even though it shows Missed.
