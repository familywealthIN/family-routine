A remote worker doesn't need a commute, but they do need what it used to provide: a cue that starts work and a cue that stops it. Without those, the laptop opens at 07:40 to check one thing and closes at 19:15 when someone asks about dinner.

Here's how one remote worker's first month with Routine Notes went once they decided to put those cues back on purpose.

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06:40 | Wake up | 5 | Glass of water |
| 07:20 | Breakfast | 5 | Kettle on |
| 08:20 | The fake commute | 10 | Shoes and jacket on |
| 08:50 | Morning focus | 30 | Headphones on |
| 12:30 | Lunch outside | 5 | Phone stays on the desk |
| 13:30 | Afternoon work | 20 | Re-read the morning's last note |
| 17:30 | Shutdown | 15 | Write tomorrow's first task |
| 18:30 | Evening for yourself | 5 | Close the office door |
| 22:30 | Night routine | 5 | Charger in the hallway |

## Week 1: why does the day have no edges?

The first week was for watching, not changing anything. Every routine went in at the time they hoped to start it. By Friday the pattern was clear. Morning focus was ticked on time every day, inside 07:50 to 09:20, but the laptop had been open since breakfast, so the tick fell in the middle of work, not at its start. Shutdown was ticked twice in five days, both times after 18:30, and its window closes at 18:00.

## Week 2: a walk that starts work

A real commute starts with shoes, so the fake one does too. Its four steps are shoes and jacket on, fifteen minutes round the block, back through the door to the desk, and today's checklist open. Step 1 is the cue, and the 08:20 reminder opens with it, not with the routine's name. Putting shoes on is easy to agree to, and once they're on, the walk usually happens.

![Home on a phone with the week strip of day rings, a work routine in progress and its five-item checklist](/screenshots/home-mobile.png)

Coming back through the door now meant work had started, and the card on Home showed it ([the Dashboard chapter](/guide/dashboard) explains the card). The breakfast email checks stopped, because work had a clear starting point.

## Week 3: the shutdown that actually shuts down

Shutdown got 15 points, as much weight as the walk and Breakfast together, and four steps: write tomorrow's first task, clear the Inbox, close every work tab, laptop in the drawer. The Inbox is the tray icon at the top of Home. Each item in it gets "Do now" or "Move to routine", so a half-finished thought gets filed under tomorrow's Morning focus instead of following you into the evening.

## Week 4: reading the record

After 30 days, More → Progress → On the clock gave the routine-by-routine picture. Morning focus and Shutdown were now mostly on time. Lunch outside was still drifting. Two out of three fixed is a good month. Lunch moved from 12:30 to 12:50, which is when it had been happening anyway, and the editor under More → Routines changed it in ten-minute steps.

## The goal the walk feeds

The fake commute turned into a year goal: walk 1,000 km over the year. That works out at roughly 85 km a month and about 20 km a week. The week milestone is five commute walks plus one long Saturday walk, with each walk as a day item linked to the commute routine.

The three pillars each have a job here. Ticking the commute inside 07:20 to 08:50 is Discipline. Working its four steps is Kinetics. Ticking the linked walk items is Geniuses. Six walks are planned against a threshold of five met days, so the week has one day of slack. A month needs three good weeks, and the 1,000 km year needs six good months.

## What happens when the walk slips?

It's pouring at 08:20 and the walk doesn't happen. There are two honest outcomes:

- **You walk at lunch instead.** The commute is in **Past** by then, so spend its 10 points to check it in that afternoon. The streak holds, and the linked walk item counts toward the week.
- **No walk at all.** Leave it. The week still has five other walks to reach its five met days.

A sick day or a holiday is different. Long-press today in the week strip and take a Skip Day: the day becomes rest, the streak survives, and you have two of them a week.

## Your next step

Add a commute routine 30 minutes before your first work block, with step 1 as shoes on. Leave everything else unchanged for seven days.
