A rotating-shift worker should keep one routine list and re-time it every time the rota turns, from earlies to lates to nights. Three separate lists sound tidier, but you'd lose the history at every change, and nobody keeps three lists up to date. With one list, the names, steps and points stay fixed. Only the times move.

## One list, two clocks

| Routine | Points | Earlies | Lates | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wake up | 5 | 04:40 | 09:00 | Glass of water |
| Travel and prep | 10 | 05:20 | 13:10 | Bag by the door |
| Shift | 35 | 06:00 | 14:00 | Locker key in hand |
| Main meal | 10 | 14:40 | 12:00 | Pan on the hob |
| Movement | 10 | 15:30 | 10:00 | Shoes on |
| Learning | 10 | 16:30 | 11:00 | Open the course |
| Unwind | 10 | 19:00 | 22:40 | Work clothes off |
| Sleep | 10 | 21:00 | 23:40 | Phone out of the bedroom |

It's 100 points on either rota. Nights work on the same principle, but a night shift is split across two calendar days because each list runs from midnight to midnight. The [night-shift post](/blog/night-shift-nurse-morning-at-seven-pm) shows how to lay that out.

## The ten-minute rota change

On the last day of a block, before the next one starts:

1. Open More → Routines, which shows the 24-hour dial and the timeline side by side.
2. Tap each routine and step its start time to the new rota. Start times move in 10-minute steps, and saving re-sorts the list and redraws the 24-hour dial.
3. Watch the dial redraw as you save, and look for gaps and overlaps. Two routines ten minutes apart is fine, because their windows overlap. A four-hour gap on a late shift is worth filling.
4. Leave the steps alone.

Step 4 is the important one.

## Why do the cues never move?

Every first step is a small physical action: a glass of water, the bag by the door, the locker key, shoes on. The reminder opens with that step. When the time moves, the cue stays exactly the same, and that's what lets the habit survive. At 04:40 or at 09:00, Wake up still starts with a glass of water. You might not know what time your body thinks it is, but your hand knows where the glass is.

## How K, D and G cope with a rota

Discipline takes the hit. Each window opens an hour before the start time and closes thirty minutes after, so the first early after a week of lates means ticking Wake up between 03:40 and 05:10. Kinetics holds steady, because the steps don't change. Geniuses depends on a goal that doesn't care what time it is.

That's why Learning runs every day on every rota. The year goal is to qualify as a team supervisor. Each month is one unit of the course. The week milestone is learning on five days, and each day item is linked to Learning. Because a week needs any five met days, a rota change on a Wednesday can't sink it. Three met weeks close the month, and six met months close the year.

## The swing day

Switching from lates to earlies, there's a day where nothing fits: you finish at 22:00 and you're due in at 06:00. When it comes, long-press today in the week strip and take a Skip Day. It's a rest day, so nothing reads as missed and the streak survives. You get two a week, and a rota turn needs one, occasionally two.

## What does a late first early look like?

Tap the previous day in the week strip to look back at it.

![A past day opened from the week strip, with D, K and G badges beside each routine and that day's checklist](/screenshots/guide/dashboard-past.jpg)

Each routine has its D, K or G badge, and the checklist shows what got done that day ([the Dashboard chapter](/guide/dashboard) explains past days). After a rota change, the pattern is always the same: two days of late ticks, then back to normal. If Wake up happened at 05:20 instead of 04:40, spend its 5 points from **Past** that same day. It's a full tick and keeps the streak. If you silenced the alarm and went back to sleep, let the miss stand.

What a month of More → Progress → On the clock should show is the gap getting shorter: three late days after the first change, two after the second, one after the third. That's real progress, even though it never looks like a perfect streak.

## Next rota change

Before you touch a single time, write down every routine's step 1. Re-time all of them without changing a step, then compare the first two days of this rota with the last one.
