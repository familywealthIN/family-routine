Rebuilding structure after burnout goes better with three routines and no heroics than with a full plan. Three small promises at times you already keep give the day a beginning, a middle and an end, and nothing more is asked.

This post is about structure, not recovery advice. If you're running on empty, rest and get support first. The app can wait.

## Why only three?

When people start again, the urge is to bring back everything at once: the early alarm, the training plan, the reading list, the side project. That's often the setup that wore them down to begin with. Each routine in Routine Notes is a promise with a time on it, and every extra promise is one more thing to fall short on. Three you can keep on a bad day.

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 08:00 | Gentle start | 35 | Open the curtains |
| 13:00 | One useful thing | 30 | Write the one thing on paper |
| 21:30 | Wind-down | 35 | Phone on the kitchen charger |

The three take the whole 100-point daily budget between them, so each tick carries real weight and three ticks make a full day. There's no long list of things left undone at the end of it.

Use the hours you already live. Someone who wakes at 07:45 is already inside the 08:00 window, which runs from 07:00 to 08:30. Starting the day with an easy win is the point.

## Steps that are almost too small

Two or three items per routine is plenty, and the first one is always something you do with your hands:

- **Gentle start:** open the curtains, drink a glass of water, ten minutes outside.
- **One useful thing:** write it on paper, do it, tick it. One thing, not a list.
- **Wind-down:** phone on the kitchen charger, a warm drink, lights low.

![Home on a phone: the week strip of day rings, the routine in progress with its checklist and one item ticked, and the Today, Week, Month and Year tabs](/screenshots/home-mobile.png)

That checklist sits on the routine's card on Home, and the reminder for each routine opens with its first item ([the Dashboard chapter](/guide/dashboard) shows the rest of the screen). Ticking the items is **Kinetics**. Turning up inside the window is **Discipline**. For the first few weeks, those two are all you're measuring.

## Let Geniuses wait

The third pillar, **Geniuses**, comes from day items linked into week, month and year goals. It's about planning, and early on you can ignore it. A week goal closes only after five good days, so setting one before the basics feel steady just adds something else to fall short of.

Once the three routines have felt ordinary for a couple of weeks, add one light goal: "Walk the river path in sections this year". Give it a month milestone ("May: two sections of the path") and a week milestone ("Walk on five days, any distance"), and link short day items like "Bridge to the lock, 2 km" to the Gentle start routine.

## What if a day falls apart?

Some days none of it happens. There are two honest responses, and a third that's fine too.

**Skip Day.** On Home, long-press today in the week strip. The day becomes a rest day, recorded as rest rather than a miss, and the streak is untouched. You get two a week. If you can tell by mid-morning that today is a rest day, skip it then, rather than watching three routines pile up in **Past**.

**A late check-in.** If the gentle start happened at 10:30 instead of 08:00, the routine is in Past because its window closed. Check it in from there that evening with points, and the streak holds. Onboarding gives you 300 welcome points, and each day's earnings are added overnight, so there's some slack. Use it for things you did late, never for things you didn't do.

**Neither.** Some days, let it go. A record with honest gaps is still useful.

## Turn the notifications down

Open **More → Notifications** and switch most of them off. Keep Good morning if seeing the day ahead helps, set to a time after you're normally up. If a nudge after a few days away would feel like pressure, switch off Welcome back. All of them can come back later.

## How do you know it's working?

Check **More → Progress** once a week, not daily. *On the clock* shows which of the three you start inside the window. If the 13:00 one keeps sliding, move it to 12:00 or make it smaller. The plan adjusts to fit your life, not the other way round.
