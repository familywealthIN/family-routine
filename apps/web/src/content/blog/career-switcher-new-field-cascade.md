Switching careers works on a cascade: one year goal, broken into certificate courses and case studies, that closes itself once enough months are met. A switch is otherwise an awkward shape, with the goal a year away, the current job filling the daytime, and progress hard to see for months. That's the kind of goal people quietly drop around week ten.

The fix is to cut the year into pieces small enough to finish this week. The example is an illustrative retail manager moving into UX research, but the structure suits any switch built on courses and a portfolio.

## What should the year goal measure?

Routine Notes can't know whether you got the job. It can see whether you did the work that gets people hired. So write the year goal as work: **"Build a UX research portfolio and start applying"**. Six months of milestones sit under it:

| Level | Example milestone |
| --- | --- |
| Year | Build a UX research portfolio and start applying |
| Month (Jan) | Certificate courses 1–2, foundations and empathy |
| Month (Feb) | Certificate courses 3–4, research methods |
| Month (Mar) | Case study 1: a local service's booking flow |
| Month (Apr) | Case study 2: five interviews, synthesised |
| Month (May) | Portfolio site live with both case studies |
| Month (Jun) | Ten applications, two coffee chats a week |

Six met months complete the year. Plan twelve if you like: the goal still closes at six. A certificate makes a good spine for the first few months because it already comes split into courses, and each course divides neatly into weeks.

## The routines that pay for it

The study has to fit around a full-time job:

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 05:50 | Wake up | 5 | Kettle on, phone stays charging |
| 06:00 | Study block | 25 | Course page open on the laptop |
| 07:30 | Get ready & commute | 5 | Bag packed the night before |
| 09:00 | Current job | 30 | Rota and floor walk |
| 18:30 | Portfolio work | 20 | Open the case-study draft |
| 21:00 | One message | 5 | Open the contacts list |
| 22:00 | Night | 10 | Laptop shut, alarm set |

The current job still has the most, 30 of the 100. It's still most of the day, and it still pays for the switch. Study and portfolio share 45, which is about right for a career change done on the side.

## One session, all three pillars

On a Tuesday in February, the day item is "Module 3.2: interview guides". It's linked to the Study block, so it appears on that card at 06:00. It sits under the week milestone "Finish module 3", which sits under February.

That one session covers the whole loop. Ticking the Study block between 05:00 and 06:30 earns **Discipline**. Working through its checklist (read, take notes, do the exercise, write one question) earns **Kinetics**. Ticking the module item earns **Geniuses**, because it's part of the plan, and plan items weigh more the higher they sit. Five good days finish the week, and three good weeks finish February.

## Re-planning in March

Around the third month, the plan will turn out to be wrong somewhere. The first case study takes twice as long as expected, or a coffee chat suggests research operations would suit you better. Change it. Open **Goals → Year Goals**, pick the goal, and rework the months that are left.

![A year goal with its progress ring, the 12-month strip and the focused month's weeks and days](/screenshots/guide/goals-cascade.jpg)

The Year Goals page shows the whole year as a 12-month strip, with the focused month's weeks and days underneath and a chat for thinking the plan through ([the Goals chapter](/guide/goals) shows how the levels connect). A plan edited three times is usually better than the January draft.

## When peak season derails the plan

Retail has peak weeks. In December the 18:30 portfolio session won't happen, and that's expected. For shifts that run until close, long-press today in the week strip and choose Skip Day (two a week, no harm to the streak), and let the week milestone wait.

If you studied, but late, say at 07:20 on the train after oversleeping, the Study block's window has closed and it's in **Past** on Home. Check it in from there before midnight with points, and you keep your run of days. A module item you forgot to tick can be ticked the next day. It will show **Missed**, but February still gets the credit.

## Start this week

Write the year goal as work, not as a job title. Add the first two month milestones from your course outline and stop there. Link this week's day items to the 06:00 Study block.
