Pairing one routine with a year goal is how a stay-at-home parent gets back to sewing. The nap-time slot gets the machine, and the goal tells each session what to make. Without the pairing, the hour fills up with laundry and the cover stays on the machine for another year.

This guide works through the setup in four steps, using a real-shaped plan.

## Step 1: give the hobby the best slot you have

Nap time is the most reliable quiet hour in the day, so the hobby gets it, ahead of the housework. In **More → Routines**, add a routine called "Sewing (nap time)" at 13:30, and make its first step the smallest physical thing that starts it: lift the cover off the machine.

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06:40 | Morning | 10 | Open the curtains |
| 08:30 | Nursery run or walk | 15 | Shoes by the door |
| 12:30 | Lunch | 10 | Plates out |
| 13:30 | Sewing (nap time) | 30 | Lift the machine cover |
| 16:00 | Play outside | 15 | Coats on |
| 19:00 | Bath and bed | 15 | Run the bath |
| 21:30 | Night routine | 5 | Phone on charge |

Sewing takes 30 of the 100 points, the largest single share. That's simply its priority. The household routines still happen, they just lose first claim on the quiet hour.

The other steps are plain: thread the machine, work on today's item, sweep up the offcuts. Starting inside the 12:30–14:00 window earns **Discipline**, and each step ticked during the slot earns **Kinetics**.

## Step 2: draft the year with the AI plan

On Home, tap the **+** beside the chat box and open the **Goal** tab, so the path is **Home → + → Goal**. Type the goal as you'd say it: *"Learn to sew my own clothes this year, roughly one garment a month."* The AI writes a plan with its own title and dated milestones. Read it through and change anything that doesn't suit you before you save. It's stored as a parent goal with the milestones beneath it.

After editing, the first half of the year might look like this:

- **January:** straight seams and a lined tote bag
- **February:** an elastic-waist skirt
- **March:** a simple boxy top
- **April:** adjusting a pattern to fit
- **May:** a shirt with buttons and buttonholes
- **June onwards:** a garment of your choice each month

## Step 3: link the days to the routine

February's week milestone, "Cut and sew the skirt", gets day items: cut the skirt pieces, sew the side seams, insert the elastic. Link each one to the Sewing routine, so the routine card shows today's job and its Week, Month and Year tabs show where it's leading.

![A year goal with its progress ring, the 12-month strip and the focused month's weeks and days](/screenshots/guide/goals-cascade.jpg)

Open the goal from Goals and its Year Goals page lays out all twelve months on one strip ([the Goals chapter](/guide/goals) explains the cascade). That linking is where **Geniuses** comes from: a ticked item under a week milestone is worth more than a loose task, and more still the further up it reaches.

## Step 4: let the generous rules work

Each week milestone needs five met days, so Monday to Friday of nap-time sewing does it. A month needs three met weeks. The year needs six met months out of the twelve you planned.

So twelve garments is the ambition and six good months is the bar. A teething fortnight in April may cost April. It won't cost the year.

## What if nap time moves late?

Some days the nap doesn't start until 15:00, an hour after the window closed. If you still sew, open the routine from **Past** on Home and spend points to check it in late. The run of sewing days stays unbroken, the tick counts fully, and the elastic you insert that afternoon still counts towards the week, even though the item shows Missed.

Some days there's no nap at all. Don't check in a session that didn't happen. Leave it in Past, or if the whole day is gone, long-press today in the week strip for **Skip Day**, one of two rest days a week that leave the streak alone.

## Re-plan rather than restart

If a garment is too ambitious, edit that month's milestone. Swap the shirt for a second skirt, or spread it over two months. Changing a milestone mid-month is Geniuses work: you're making the plan fit the year you're actually having.

## Your next step

Pick the hobby, give it your best quiet hour with at least 20 points, and type the goal into the AI plan tonight. Tomorrow at nap time, all you have to do is lift the cover.
