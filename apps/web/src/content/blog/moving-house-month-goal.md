Moving house in a month works best as one month goal with four week milestones under it, worked in a 30-point evening move block. The month closes once three of those weeks are met, so moving week can go off plan without sinking the goal. Kept in your head, a move is a month of background worry. Laid out like this, it's a list you can watch get shorter.

Here's one move, a week at a time.

## The setup: one routine and one goal

First, open **More → Routines** and add a routine for the move itself, at the time you'll actually work on it. For most people with a job, that's straight after dinner.

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06:40 | Morning | 10 | Glass of water |
| 07:30 | Breakfast | 5 | Kettle on |
| 09:00 | Work | 40 | Notebook open |
| 18:30 | Move block | 30 | Build two boxes |
| 21:00 | Admin ten | 10 | Open the Inbox |
| 22:00 | Night routine | 5 | Phone on charge |

That's 100 in total. The move block gets 30 because it's this month's priority, and it's the routine most likely to slide quietly into "tomorrow". Its first step is building two boxes, which you can do even when you're exhausted, and once there are empty boxes in the room, filling them is the obvious next move.

Then add the month goal, "Moved in and set up by the 30th", with one week milestone for each week. Under each week go the day items, linked to Move block so they appear on its card.

Add each week's items on the first day of that week. A past day can't take new items, so an item needs to exist before its day arrives. Anything dated ahead goes under **Plan** on Priority.

## Week 1: sort and clear

The milestone is "Every room sorted into keep, give and bin". The day items take one area per evening: kitchen cupboards on Monday, the wardrobe on Tuesday, the loft on Wednesday. Each item has its own sub-tasks. Kitchen cupboards might be: empty the top shelf, check the tins, fill the charity box.

Sub-tasks you clear in the slot earn **Kinetics**. The item itself sits under a week and a month milestone, which is where **Geniuses** comes in.

## Week 2: pack the rarely used, start the admin

The milestone is "Everything we won't need this fortnight is boxed". In parallel, the 21:00 admin slot handles the paperwork: change-of-address forms, moving the broadband, booking the van. For calls, press the **+** beside the chat box and type "Book the van on Friday at 9am", and the date and time are filled in for you.

## Week 3: moving week, when the plan breaks

This is the week that falls apart, so its milestone is modest on purpose: "Moved, with the essentials box unpacked first". On moving day, long-press today in the week strip and pick **Skip Day**. A move is exactly what the week's two rest days are for, and taking one leaves the streak alone.

On the evenings either side, if the move block starts at 21:30 instead of 18:30, it's already in **Past** by then. Open it there and spend points to check it in late. The run of days holds, and the items you tick still count towards the week, even marked Missed.

## Week 4: set up the rooms

The milestone is "Every room usable". The day items: beds made, kitchen unpacked, a lamp in every room, the last box flattened.

## Why does the month close anyway?

![Goals with the period ladder, the month calendar of day rings and today's goals grouped by routine](/screenshots/guide/goals-calendar.jpg)

The month calendar on Goals shows each evening's ring filling up, so a blank stretch stands out straight away ([the Goals chapter](/guide/goals) explains the ladder). A week milestone completes once five of its dates have every linked item ticked, and the month completes once three of its four week milestones are met.

So if moving week collapses, and it usually does, weeks 1, 2 and 4 still close the month. The fourth week was ambition. Three was always the bar.

If you'd like the move to sit inside something bigger, put the month under a year goal such as "Settled into the new place". That year needs six met months, and this is one of them.
