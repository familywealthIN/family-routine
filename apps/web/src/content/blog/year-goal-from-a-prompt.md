To turn a vague ambition into a year plan, write the AI plan's prompt like a brief for a coach: the outcome, the deadline, where you're starting and what limits you. The AI then writes dated milestones and a title of its own, you edit them, and the plan saves as a parent goal with its milestones underneath.

"Get fit", "learn to code" and "write more" are ambitions, not plans. You can't schedule them, and in March you can't tell whether you're on track. The usual fix is an afternoon with a spreadsheet, and most people never get round to it. The AI plan does the slicing, and you stay in charge.

## Where is the AI plan?

On Home, tap the **+** beside the chat box: **Home → + → Goal**. The add box opens with three tabs, Task, Goal and Search, and the Goal tab is the AI plan. What you type there is a prompt, not the goal's name.

## What makes a good prompt?

Give it four things:

1. **An outcome you can check:** *run a half marathon*, not *get fit*.
2. **A deadline:** *by the end of October*.
3. **Your starting point:** *I can run 5 km without stopping*.
4. **Your constraints:** *three runs a week; I'm away most of August*.

Put together:

> Run a half marathon by the end of October. I can run 5 km now. Three runs a week, Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday. I'm away for most of August, so keep it light.

The plan comes back with a title of its own, something like *Half marathon build: 5 km to 21.1 km*, a description, and a run of dated month milestones, roughly one a month from January to October. The AI's title is usually tidier than one typed in a hurry, and you can still edit it before saving, along with everything else.

## Edit before you save

Read the milestones as a coach's plan and check three numbers:

- **At least 6 months.** A year goal closes once 6 month milestones are met. Ten planned months leaves 4 spare. Fewer than 6 can never close.
- **3 real weeks a month.** A month closes at 3 met weeks. If you're away for 3 weeks in August, a hard August milestone can't close, so move it.
- **A test at the end.** Good plans finish on something checkable: a race, a time trial, finished work.

## Wire it to a routine

A plan without a routine is a nice document. Link the goal and its milestones to the routine that will do the work, with the same routine link every goal item has. The routine's editor then shows the year goal it serves.

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 | Linked year goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06:30 | Wake up | 5 | Fill a glass of water | None |
| 06:50 | Run | 20 | Lace the running shoes | Half marathon build |
| 07:40 | Mobility | 5 | Unroll the mat | Half marathon build |
| 08:30 | Work | 40 | Close every tab but one | None |
| 18:30 | Strength | 10 | Bag by the door | Half marathon build |
| 21:00 | Family time | 10 | Clear the table | None |
| 22:30 | Wind-down | 10 | Phone on charge outside the bedroom | None |

That's 100 points. Run carries 20 because this year it's the point. Its window runs from 05:50 to 07:20, so a 06:50 start still fits a 07:40 mobility slot.

**Goals → Year Goals** shows the saved plan as a 12-month strip with a focus card for the current month and its own chat ([the Goals chapter](/guide/goals) shows every view). The chat is a good place to ask what this month still needs.

## Let the weeks come from the month

Don't pre-write 52 week milestones. Each Monday, read the month milestone and write one week under it, for example *three runs, the long one at 12 km*, with day items on Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday. Then the arithmetic runs itself: 5 met days close the week, 3 met weeks close March, and 6 met months close the year. Each tick on a linked item earns Geniuses, and longer horizons weigh more.

## What happens when a month slips?

At some point one will. Say August was meant to be light and turned into nothing at all.

- **Late ticks count.** Sunday's run, ticked on Tuesday, still moves the week. The status says *Missed* because it records timing, but the progress is real.
- **Missed routines can be rescued that day.** If Run's window closes at 07:20 unticked, check it in late from **Past** with its 20 points before midnight. The streak holds and its agents run.
- **Re-plan, don't restart.** Rewrite September's milestone to absorb the gap. With 10 months planned and 6 needed, one lost month still leaves 3 spare.

Write one prompt tonight, for the goal you've been saying out loud for a year. Edit the plan, link it to one routine, and write this week's milestone.
