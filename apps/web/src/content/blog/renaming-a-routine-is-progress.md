Renaming a routine is progress when it comes with a rebuild: a night routine that stopped working mid-season gets a new name, regrouped steps and a sharper first cue. The first version of any routine is a guess. "Night routine" at 22:30, with four steps written on the first evening: brush teeth, phone off, read, sleep. Six weeks in, the phone goes off at 23:40, the reading never happens, and the routine gets checked in late or not at all.

The tempting response is to try harder. The better one is to rebuild. In the KDG framework that's what **Geniuses** means: improving the plan so the next loop starts from a better place.

## What does the data say before you touch anything?

Look first. **More → Progress** has an *On the clock* view showing on-time against late check-ins for each routine. Then look back at a few recent evenings.

![A past day opened from the week strip, showing that day's checklist marked past and a chat offering What did I do last time?](/screenshots/guide/dashboard-past.jpg)

Tap an earlier day in the week strip to see which items actually got ticked, and ask the routine's chat "What did I do last time?" if it has an `area:` or `project:` tag ([the Dashboard chapter](/guide/dashboard) covers past days). Usually one of three things is wrong:

- **The cue is wrong.** "Brush teeth" isn't a starting cue. It's something you already do automatically.
- **The order is wrong.** Reading comes before the phone goes away, so it never happens.
- **The routine does two jobs.** Getting ready for bed and getting ready for tomorrow are different tasks, and one list can't hold both.

## The rebuild

The illustrative evening, before:

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19:30 | Workout | 10 | Gym bag on the shoulder |
| 21:00 | Family time | 5 | Clear the table |
| 22:30 | Night routine | 5 | Brush teeth |

And after:

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 19:30 | Workout | 10 | Gym bag on the shoulder |
| 21:00 | Family time | 5 | Clear the table |
| 22:00 | Tomorrow, ready | 5 | Bag packed by the door |
| 22:30 | Screens off & wind-down | 5 | Phone on the charger in the hall |

The evening went from 20 points to 25. The other 75 stay with the morning and work routines, and the new 22:00 slot takes 5 from a morning routine that didn't need them. The editor in **More → Routines** won't let the day go over 100, and it shows what's left when you add a routine.

Three changes, each doing a specific job:

1. **Split the two jobs.** "Tomorrow, ready" covers the bag, clothes, a look at tomorrow's list and the next day's first task. The bedtime routine is only for winding down.
2. **Rename it after the outcome.** "Night routine" doesn't say what it's for. "Screens off & wind-down" does, and the name is the first thing you see on the card and in the reminder.
3. **Make step 1 physical.** "Phone on the charger in the hall" is an action. Once the phone has left the room, the rest of the list tends to follow, and the 22:30 reminder now opens with it.

## How many steps is too many?

Rebuilt routines tend to grow, and a fifteen-step bedtime list is easy to end up with: brush, floss, face wash, journal, tomorrow's tasks, stretch, and so on. Every ticked item earns **Kinetics**, but a list that long gets skimmed, not followed. A useful rule: if an item never gets ticked, delete it or move it to the routine where it actually happens. Face and teeth belong together. Reviewing tomorrow's tasks belongs in "Tomorrow, ready".

## What happens to the goals

Day items already linked to the routine keep their place in the plan. Week, month and year milestones don't depend on a routine's name. The week goal "Screens off by 23:00 on five nights" still completes once five nights are met. Link new day items, such as "Phone in the hall by 22:40", to the renamed routine and they'll appear on its card.

The rebuild doesn't earn points by itself. Geniuses points come from ticking linked items, and the further up the plan they sit, the more they weigh. What the rebuild does is make those ticks likely again. A routine checked in on time five nights a week does more for the year goal than one you keep meaning to fix.

## When the rebuilt routine slips in week one

Give it about two weeks before judging it, and expect a few evenings in **Past** along the way. If you did the routine but after 23:00, when its window closed, check it in from Past before midnight with points, and the run of nights holds. If a night out means no routine at all, let it go, or, before midnight, long-press today in the week strip for a Skip Day if the whole day was a write-off.

After fourteen days, check *On the clock* again. If the new routine is on time most nights, the rebuild worked. If not, rebuild it again. Milestones get re-planned, not restarted.
