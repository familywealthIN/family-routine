A video creator can build a one-video-a-week publishing cadence on the five-day rule: seven planned stages, and the week closes once five days are met. Creators who stop publishing usually don't run out of motivation. They lose the week. A video is five or six separate jobs, and when all of them sit under "make video", it's Thursday and nothing has been filmed.

## The routines

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 07:00 | Wake up | 5 | Glass of water |
| 07:30 | Walk and ideas | 10 | Voice memo app open |
| 09:00 | Studio block | 35 | Studio light on |
| 13:00 | Lunch | 5 | Away from the edit |
| 14:00 | Edit and admin | 25 | Open the project file |
| 17:30 | Community | 10 | Open the comments |
| 22:00 | Night routine | 10 | Write tomorrow's stage down |

Seven routines, 100 points. Studio block gets the most because a video that isn't filmed can't be edited, however much editing time there is. Its five steps, set under More → Routines, are the filming day in order: studio light on, read the script aloud once, main take, cutaways, back up the cards. The 09:00 reminder opens with the light switch, which is easier to agree to than "film the video".

## The week goal, one stage per day

The week milestone is "This week's video live". Under it, each day gets one item, linked to the routine where that stage happens:

| Day | Day item | Linked routine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | Research and outline | Walk and ideas |
| Tue | Script, final draft | Studio block |
| Wed | Film the main take | Studio block |
| Thu | Rough cut | Edit and admin |
| Fri | Final cut and thumbnail | Edit and admin |
| Sat | Publish and pin a comment | Community |
| Sun | Reply to the first comments | Community |

![The focused routine card switched to the Week tab, showing the week goal and its linked day goals](/screenshots/guide/dashboard-cascade.jpg)

On Home, the routine card's Week tab shows the week milestone with its stages underneath ([the Dashboard chapter](/guide/dashboard) explains the tabs). Seven stages against a five-day threshold gives two days of slack. That isn't lowering the bar. Planning seven is ambition, and the week still closes at five.

## Why one item per day?

A day only counts once every item linked to the week on that date is ticked. If Thursday had both "Rough cut" and "Colour grade", a finished rough cut wouldn't be enough to make Thursday count. Splitting stages across days keeps each date simple: one tick, one met day.

## Up the cascade

The year goal is fifty videos. Each month milestone is four videos out, one of them long-form, and it needs three published weeks out of four or five, so one bad week doesn't sink the month. The year needs six good months out of twelve. Every stage ticked inside this cascade earns Geniuses on top of the routine's points.

If you don't want to build the cascade by hand, open the add box with **+**, choose the Goal tab, and type something like "Publish a video every week this year with one long-form piece a month". The AI writes the plan, including its own title and dated milestones. Edit them to fit your real schedule before you save.

## When a stage slips

The filming location falls through on Wednesday. There are two good responses:

- **Move it.** Open Wednesday's "Film the main take" and change its date to Thursday. Thursday now needs both of its items ticked, but the week still has six dates that can be met.
- **Let it go.** If nothing moves, the week still has six dates left, and it only needs five.

If filming happens late on Wednesday, after Studio block's window (08:00 to 09:30) has closed, spend its 35 points from **Past** that day to check it in. The streak holds, and the tick counts in full. A stage finished on Friday but ticked on Saturday still counts as well. It reads Missed, but the week still moves.

Travelling to a shoot and none of the routines fit? Long-press today in the week strip and take a Skip Day. You get two a week, and a skipped day doesn't break the streak.

## This week

Make next week's video a week milestone with seven one-line stages, one for each day. On Sunday, count how many of the seven days were met. Five is a published week.
