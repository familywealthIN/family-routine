A parent of two can run school-run mornings as one chain of short routines between 06:20 and 08:40. The windows overlap, so each tick hands you the next card, and most of the 07:40 decisions get made the night before. Here's how one morning goes, routine by routine.

## The chain

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06:20 | Up before the house | 10 | Kettle on |
| 07:00 | Wake the kids | 10 | Open both curtains |
| 07:20 | Breakfast | 10 | Bowls on the table |
| 07:50 | Bags and shoes | 15 | Bags by the door |
| 08:10 | School run | 15 | Keys in hand |
| 09:00 | Work | 30 | Notebook open |
| 21:00 | Reset for tomorrow | 10 | Lunchboxes on the counter |

Together these use the full 100 points. The heaviest morning weights, 15 each, sit on bags-and-shoes and the school run, because those are the two that make you late.

## 06:20: ten quiet minutes

The first routine is yours: kettle on, a glass of water, a look at today's list before anyone needs you. Its window runs from 05:20 to 06:50, so even a slow start is on time. It's the easiest Discipline of the day, and it sets the pace for everything after.

## Why do the 07:00–07:50 windows overlap?

Each routine's window opens an hour before its start and closes half an hour after. So Wake the kids (06:00–07:30), Breakfast (06:20–07:50) and Bags and shoes (06:50–08:20) are all open at 07:20. You don't have to wait for one routine to end before the next is allowed. If breakfast is quick, tick it and go straight to bags.

The focused card moves on to whatever is due as you tick ([the Dashboard chapter](/guide/dashboard) shows the layout). Keep each checklist to three steps. Breakfast: bowls out, cereal or toast, plates in the sink. Bags and shoes: bags by the door, shoes on, reading folders checked. Three you finish beat eight you skim.

## 08:10: out of the door

The school-run window closes at 08:40. Its only real step is leaving: keys in hand, door shut. Tick it from the pavement and the morning's Discipline is done.

## Why does the 21:00 reset matter most?

The evening reset is worth 10 points, and it's what holds the morning together. Lunchboxes on the counter, uniforms laid out, bags by the door, tomorrow's list checked. Each of those is one less decision at 07:40.

You'll see it in the record. On mornings after a skipped reset, Bags and shoes is the routine that most often ends up in **Past**.

## Pairing the chain with one goal

Give the chain one goal to feed: "Calm mornings: out by 08:15 most school days", with November as the month milestone and "Out by 08:15 on five school days" as the week milestone. Each daily item is linked to the School run routine.

A school week has exactly five days, and five met days finish a week goal, so one clean week closes it. If Wednesday's item gets ticked at 09:30 from the office, it shows Missed but still counts. Three good school weeks close November. Six good months close the year, which leaves the holidays out of the maths.

## What if the morning breaks anyway?

- **Late, not lost.** The phone was left in the car and you only notice at 09:30. Open School run in **Past** and spend points to check it in late. The run of mornings carries on.
- **Not a school day.** For a staff training day or a sick day at home, long-press today in the week strip and pick **Skip Day**. It's one of the week's two rest days, and no streak is lost.
- **Half-term.** A whole week off is more than two Skip Days can cover. Go to **More → Routines**, rename School run to "Out of the house" for the week so the slot still means something, and change it back when term starts.
