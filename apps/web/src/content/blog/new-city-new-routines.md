For the first 30 days after a move to a new city, use your routines to rebuild your anchors before you try anything else. A move takes away the gym you went to without thinking, the café that knew your order and the route that let you plan the day on autopilot, and getting those fixed points back is what makes a strange place feel like home.

Here's the month, week by week, in Routine Notes.

## Week 1: put the anchors back

Add nothing new in the first week. Set up the routines that used to run without effort, at times you'll actually keep in the new place:

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 07:00 | Wake & window | 10 | Open the curtains |
| 07:30 | Run the neighbourhood | 15 | Shoes on, keys in pocket |
| 08:40 | Work start | 30 | Laptop on the new desk |
| 12:30 | Lunch somewhere new | 10 | Pick a street you haven't walked |
| 18:30 | Shop & cook | 10 | Check what's in the fridge |
| 19:30 | Find your people | 15 | Open one listings page |
| 22:00 | Wind-down | 10 | Unpack one box |

That's exactly 100. Work start gets 30, the biggest share, because it drifts most while the commute is still new. Find your people gets 15 because it's the one you'll put off longest.

Work start's window opens at 07:40 and closes at 09:10. Arriving at the new desk inside that window, every weekday, is the whole job this week. It earns **Discipline**: the same time, in a place where nothing else is the same yet.

## Week 2: make the steps local

The routines exist now, but their checklists are still generic. Rewrite them for the streets you live on. Run the neighbourhood becomes: shoes on, keys in pocket, the river loop, back past the bakery. Lunch somewhere new becomes: pick a street, eat there, and if the place is worth going back to, add it to the day item's notes.

Ticking those items earns **Kinetics**. A step that names a real place is far easier to start than "go for a run", and the routine's reminder opens with the first step, so at 07:30 the phone says "Shoes on, keys in pocket".

## Week 3: one month goal

Now add a plan. One month goal is enough: **"Know the city"**, with week milestones that build on each other:

- Week 1: a gym, a supermarket and a café you'd go back to.
- Week 2: join one group, such as a running club, a class or a language exchange.
- Week 3: three routes you can walk without the map.
- Week 4: invite someone from the group to something.

Each week gets day items linked to a routine. "Try the Tuesday club run" goes under Find your people. "Walk to work without the map" goes under Work start. Ticking linked items earns **Geniuses**.

![The Goals page switched to the week, listing this week's goals by routine with the progress of each](/screenshots/guide/goals-week.jpg)

Switch Goals to the week view to see how far "Join one group" has got ([the Goals chapter](/guide/goals) covers the views). A week is complete once five of its days are met, and the month after three good weeks, so if week 4 never happens, the month still closes. If it's working, add a year goal above it, "Have a life here: three regular places, one group". The month becomes its first milestone, and six good months will complete it.

## Week 4: what does the data say?

Open **More → Progress**, pick the Week view, and read *On the clock*. A move usually looks the same in the data: work start on time, evening routines slipping. That isn't a character flaw. It's the new commute, the new shop's opening hours, and the fact that you don't know anyone yet.

Change the plan to fit. If the shop shuts at 19:00, Shop & cook should start at 17:50. In **More → Routines**, tap the routine, adjust its start time in 10-minute steps, and check on the 24-hour dial that it still fits.

## When the move knocks you off track

Some days the move swallows whole: waiting in for a delivery, signing the lease, a second weekend of flat-pack furniture. On Home, long-press today in the week strip and mark it as a **Skip Day**. It's counted as rest, not as a gap, and you get two a week.

A routine that just ran late is different. The 07:30 run that happened at 09:15, because the boiler engineer came early, sits in **Past** once its window has closed. Before the day ends, check it in from there with points, and your run of days is safe. The 300 welcome points from onboarding cover a rough first month.

## Day 31

You'll know the routines are working when Lunch somewhere new keeps taking you back to the same place on purpose. When that happens, rename it. You're a local now.
