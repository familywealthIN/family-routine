A planning routine that keeps slipping is usually a short, low-point slot with no physical cue, wedged between two routines that matter more. There are four ways to fix it: merge it into the next routine, move it to the night before, anchor it to something physical, or let the app draft the plan for you.

On **More → Progress**, one routine often stands out, and it's rarely the run or the big work block. It's the ten-minute *Plan the day* at 08:00, between breakfast and work. On busy mornings it goes first, and after a couple of months it's the routine you miss most. It's worth fixing, because planning is where Geniuses starts: it's when day items get linked to the week milestones above them.

## Why does it slip?

Here's the morning around it. The evening routines hold the other 40 of the day's 100 points.

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 07:00 | Run | 10 | Lace the running shoes |
| 07:40 | Breakfast & get ready | 10 | Blend the shake |
| 08:00 | Plan the day | 5 | Open today's list |
| 08:30 | Deep work | 35 | Clear chats and reviews |

Three things work against it:

1. **No cue.** "Open today's list" is a screen action, and a phone at 08:00 is a trap. There's nothing to pick up.
2. **Overlapping windows.** Plan the day's window runs from 07:00 to 08:30. Deep work's opens at 07:30. At 08:05 you can skip straight to Deep work and still be on time for the routine worth 35 points.
3. **Low stakes.** At 5 points, a missed planning slot barely dents the D ring. Points tell the app what matters, and they tell you too.

You can rescue it from **Past** the same day by spending its 5 points. The streak holds and any agent on it still runs. But rescuing it three mornings a week means the design is the problem. Try one of these fixes at a time, two weeks each.

![Home on a phone with the week strip, the Start Work routine in progress and its five-item checklist with one step ticked](/screenshots/home-mobile.png)

All four fixes change what that card shows at 08:00 ([the Dashboard chapter](/guide/dashboard) covers the card and its checklist).

## Fix 1: merge it into the next routine

Delete *Plan the day* and make planning **step 1 of Deep work**: "Write today's three tasks on the card." Deep work goes from 35 to 40 points.

Planning can't be skipped now without skipping the work block's checklist, and finishing it earns Kinetics inside a routine you never miss. The overlap that used to hurt becomes the reason it works.

## Fix 2: move it to the night before

If mornings are full, plan at 22:30. Add "Choose tomorrow's first three items" as a step in the night routine, and add them to tomorrow's routines while you're there. When you wake, each item is already on its routine card and linked to its week milestone, so the morning starts from a plan instead of a blank list.

## Fix 3: give it a physical cue

Keep the routine but change step 1 to something you can hold: "Sit at the table with the notebook open." Switch its reminder from **Notify** to **Alarm** in the routine's editor, so it rings at exactly 08:00 and again at 08:05 if not done. Raise it to 10 points, taking 5 from somewhere that matters less, so the D ring notices a miss.

## Fix 4: let the app draft the plan

Turn on **Day planning** under **More → Notifications**. When nothing is planned yet, it suggests tasks from your week. Or open the routine's chat on Home. Its *Before you start* brief lists up to 3 next steps for each `area:` or `project:` tag on the routine, each with an **Add** button, and the *Plan from next steps* reply turns them into a plan. Planning becomes reviewing a draft: two minutes instead of ten.

## How do you know the fix worked?

After two weeks, read *On the clock* for the routine that now carries planning. On-time check-ins should outnumber late ones, say 8 of 10 workdays against 4 of 10 before. Then check your week goals. A planning fix that works shows up as more met days, because each day's items were linked and ticked, not improvised. Remember the rules while you judge it: a week closes at 5 met days, a late tick on a goal item still counts even though it reads *Missed*, and a month needs 3 met weeks, so one rough Monday doesn't sink it.

A routine that keeps slipping is information, not a character flaw. The fix is almost never "try harder at 08:00". It's usually a better cue, a better time, or a better place in the chain.
