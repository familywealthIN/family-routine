Getting back to training after months off goes best as 30 days of sessions so small they never feel worth skipping. The old you is the problem: you remember the distances and weights from before the break, and the gap between that and today makes every session feel like a failure. So the first month isn't about catching up with that person. It's about turning up.

## Why only 10 points?

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 (the cue) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06:40 | Wake up | 10 | Open the curtains |
| 07:00 | Move | 10 | Change into kit |
| 07:40 | Breakfast | 10 | Kettle on |
| 09:00 | Work | 40 | Open today's list |
| 13:00 | Walk | 10 | Coat on, out the door |
| 19:00 | Dinner & family | 10 | Lay the table |
| 22:00 | Night routine | 10 | Kit out on the chair |

The day adds up to 100, and Move gets 10 of it on purpose. A routine's points set how much it can earn and also what a late rescue costs. At 10, a bad morning is cheap to recover and doesn't dominate the day. Training gets more weight once it has shown it can carry some.

## Days 1–10: make the steps tiny

Open Move from **More → Routines** and cut the suggested steps down to four:

1. Change into kit
2. Walk for ten minutes
3. Two rounds of the basics you used to do
4. Note one thing that felt good

That's the whole session. Ticking each step earns Kinetics: a small amount of work, done properly. If you feel like doing more, add it with the **+** as a quick task in the Move slot. It counts, but it isn't the plan, so skipping it tomorrow isn't a miss.

![The add box open over Home, adding a quick task to the routine that is running now](/screenshots/guide/dashboard-quicktask.jpg)

The add box files a bonus set inside the routine you're already in ([the Dashboard chapter](/guide/dashboard)).

Move's window runs from 06:00 to 07:30. A tick inside it earns Discipline. Leave the reminder on **Notify** for now. The aim this month is to find when training actually fits, not to force it.

## Days 11–20: the first miss

Some morning in this stretch will get away from you, whether you oversleep or the day starts with someone else's emergency. If you made the session up at 17:00, Move is in **Past** on Home. Tick it there that evening for 10 points and the streak carries on. Your 300 welcome points from onboarding cover thirty of those, and what you earn each day settles overnight.

If nothing happened at all, leave the day blank, and don't do a double session tomorrow to make up for it. Move stays the same size every day. A blank day is just information.

On day 20, open **More → Progress** and look at *On the clock*. If Move was late on 5 of the last 10 days, try running it in the 13:00 Walk slot for a week. If it was mostly on time, leave it where it is.

## Days 21–30: give it a direction

Now the habit can have a goal, small and close at hand:

- **Month:** "Five sessions a week, every week this month"
- **Week:** "Five sessions", with five dated items linked to Move
- **Day:** "Walk 10 minutes plus two rounds"

The week closes on its fifth met date, so five planned sessions are enough. You don't need seven. Each item sits on the Move card under the Week tab, and ticking it now earns Geniuses, because it belongs to something bigger than one morning.

There's still no year goal at this point. Add one when month two starts, once you know what four weeks of turning up looks like.

## What changes on day 31?

Move gets more weight, say 15 or 20 points, taken from a routine that's been coasting. The editor shows what's left of the 100 as you change things. Add one step to the session. Leave the cue alone: changing into kit worked for thirty days and there's no reason to touch it now.
