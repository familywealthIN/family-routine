A sales rep who is on time but stuck needs to rebuild the calling routine around the first dial, not try to show up harder. In this case More → Progress made it obvious: Discipline was high almost every day, because First dial was ticked inside its window each morning, but Kinetics hardly moved. The routine wasn't recording any of the actual work.

This is a troubleshooting post. The symptom is strong D with weak K, and the cause is nearly always the same.

## Why is Kinetics flat when Discipline is high?

Because the steps were labels. The old First dial routine had three: "Calls", "CRM" and "Email". None of them can be finished. "Calls" was either ticked in a rush at 12:15 or not ticked at all. Kinetics comes from working the routine's checklist and the quick tasks done in its slot, so a checklist of labels gives it nothing to count.

## Fix 1: steps that are call blocks

The new checklist follows the morning as it actually happens:

1. Headset on
2. Block one: ten dials
3. Log every outcome
4. Block two: ten dials
5. Log every outcome
6. Send the two warmest follow-ups

The reminder at 08:40 opens with step 1, because putting a headset on is easier to agree to than "start calling". Each block takes about forty minutes, so by 10:00 two steps are done and Kinetics is building while the morning is still going.

## Fix 2: follow-ups as quick tasks

Calls produce promises: send the case study, call back on Thursday. Each one goes in through **+** as soon as it's made. Typing "Call back the warehouse lead Thursday at 3pm" fills in the date and time. Anything for today lands in the current slot and adds Kinetics when it's done. Anything dated for a later day moves to Plan.

## Fix 3: hand off what isn't yours

About half the follow-up list was other people's work: a contract to send, a demo account to set up.

![The four Priority quadrants, Do now, Plan, Delegate and Automate, with day goals grouped by routine](/screenshots/guide/priority-quadrants.jpg)

@mention the colleague who owns the item and it moves to Delegate on its own ([the Priority chapter](/guide/priority) explains how items are sorted). The rep's rule is three items or fewer in Do now. With a fourth, the afternoon plan has already slipped.

## Fix 4: link inputs, not outcomes

The year goal is to move up from inside sales to account executive. The month milestone, forty discovery meetings booked, is an outcome, and nobody controls whether a prospect picks up. So the week and day levels track inputs instead: the week milestone is "Full call blocks every working day", and each day item is "Twenty dials, all logged", linked to First dial.

That choice matters because of the arithmetic. A calling week has five working days, and a week closes at five met days, so every day has to be met. With "book a meeting" as the day item, one quiet Tuesday would sink the week. Twenty logged dials is still possible on a bad day. The forty meetings get counted at the month level, which only needs three of its weeks met. Ticking those linked items earns Geniuses.

## What if First dial misses its window?

A team meeting takes the first hour, and First dial's window, which runs from 07:40 to 09:10, closes with nothing ticked.

- **The blocks happened from 10:00.** Spend the routine's 30 points from **Past** to check it in that day. It's a full tick, and the streak holds.
- **No calls happened.** Leave the routine and the day item unticked.
- **The dials happened but the item wasn't ticked.** Tick it the next morning. It reads Missed, but the week still counts it.

## Check again in fourteen days

After two weeks, look at the Kinetics total on Progress again, day by day. It should now be climbing, and a day of checklist ticks should show up as Kinetics on that day. If it's still flat, go through the checklist one more time: any step that's still a label is the problem.
