Your first job's first year goes better with three year goals and a cascade to match them: one goal for the job, one for a skill and one for a habit. The first year already brings plenty of goals of its own (learn the systems, learn the people, get through the first big meeting), but none of them tell you whether you're improving or just getting busier.

The Geniuses pillar of the KDG framework fills that gap. It rewards linking each day to week, month and year goals, so the grind adds up to something you can see.

## Which three year goals?

One per area:

1. **The job:** "Be trusted with a project of my own by December."
2. **A skill:** "Get properly good at SQL."
3. **A habit:** "Exercise four times a week, every week."

Three covers work, growth and health without the Goals page turning into a second backlog. Each goal gets a routine, so the plan and the day are connected.

## A day built around them

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06:40 | Wake up | 10 | Water by the bed |
| 07:30 | Commute: read | 10 | Book out before the doors close |
| 08:50 | Deep work | 30 | Open today's ticket |
| 12:30 | Lunch walk | 10 | Jacket on, phone in pocket |
| 17:30 | Skill hour | 20 | Open the practice database |
| 21:00 | Plan tomorrow | 10 | Open tomorrow's list |
| 22:30 | Night | 10 | Phone across the room |

Deep work takes 30 of the 100 points. It's most of the day, and it's where the job goal will be won or lost. The skill hour takes 20 because evening study is the first thing to go when a release runs late. The five anchor routines at 10 each hold the edges of the day in place.

## How does a Tuesday exercise reach the year?

Take the skill goal:

- **Year:** "Get properly good at SQL."
- **Month:** "March: joins, grouping and window functions."
- **Week:** "Window functions module, plus one rewritten report."
- **Day:** "Exercises 1–8: ranking in groups", linked to Skill hour.

Because it's linked to the routine, the day item turns up on the Skill hour card at 17:30, and the card's Week and Month tabs show what it belongs to. If you start before 18:00, the routine's tick earns **Discipline**. Working through its checklist earns **Kinetics**. Ticking the exercise earns **Geniuses**, and items further up the plan earn more.

The goals above complete themselves. With five good days the week is done, three good weeks finish March, and six good months finish the year. You never tick the year goal yourself. Your days do it.

## Re-planning in May

By May you'll know more about the job than you did in January. The project you hoped to lead may not exist, or a better one may have turned up. Change the remaining month milestones to fit. Re-planning mid-year isn't backing down. It's the "improve the plan" step of the loop, and the points arrive when the new plan's items get ticked.

For the job goal, the AI plan is a good place to start. On Home, tap the **+** beside the chat box and open the Goal tab. Describe what you want ("Become the person my team trusts to run a small project by December"), then edit the dated milestones it suggests before saving.

## The weeks that go wrong

Sooner or later there's a launch or an offsite, and the skill hour misses four nights in a row. Three things limit the damage.

Late ticks count. Did the exercises at 23:00 and forgot to tick them? Tick them over breakfast. The item will show **Missed**, because the label is about timing, but the week still moves forward.

A routine you started late can still be checked in. Sit down at 19:40, after the 18:00 cut-off, and the Skill hour will have moved to **Past** on Home. Check it in from there before midnight with points, and the streak holds. Earnings are added overnight, so good weeks pay for bad ones.

A day lost entirely to travel for the offsite is a Skip Day: long-press today in the week strip. You get two a week, and they don't count against you.

## A fifteen-minute review each month

On the last Sunday of the month, open **More → Progress**, switch to Month, and look at which of the three areas moved. *On the clock* shows which routines slid.

![The Goals page with the period ladder, the month calendar of day rings and today's goals grouped by routine](/screenshots/guide/goals-calendar.jpg)

Then open Goals and read the month calendar. Any blank fortnight shows up straight away ([the Goals chapter](/guide/goals) explains the rings). Adjust next month's milestones and add the first week's day items. Anything dated ahead files itself under Plan in [Priority](/guide/priority). Twelve of these reviews will tell you more about your first year than the appraisal at the end of it.
