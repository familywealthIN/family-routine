A night-shift nurse should set routines by the clock, not by the shift, and accept that morning comes at 15:00. Lunch is at 23:30 and bedtime is after everyone else has gone to work. Routine Notes handles this fine once you know one thing about how it counts days.

## How does a night shift fit a calendar day?

Each day's routine list runs from midnight to midnight, on the calendar. A shift doesn't, so one shift is split across two lists. The routines that start the shift are on Monday's list, and the wind-down and sleep that follow are on Tuesday's.

It sounds awkward, but it reads well. Tuesday's list, from top to bottom, says: finish Monday's shift, sleep, wake, start Tuesday's shift. The day still has a shape. It just begins where the last shift ended.

Nine routines, 100 points, all set in More → Routines in the order of a calendar day, not the order of the shift.

## Why is the wind-down the anchor?

After twelve hours on the ward, the morning is where things fall apart: a quick scroll turns into an hour, and sleep starts late. So the wind-down gets four physical steps: uniform in the wash, shower, blinds down, phone charging in another room. The reminder opens with step 1, because you can put a uniform in the wash while still wearing your coat.

The routine starts at 08:30, so its window runs from 07:30 to 09:00. That's enough slack for a handover that overruns by half an hour, but not enough to let the scroll win.

## How K, D and G show up

- **Discipline** is ticking inside each window. Shift start at 19:00 is the easiest tick of the day, because the rota sets that time. Wake up at 15:00 and Walk outside at 15:40 are the ones that take effort.
- **Kinetics** comes from the checklists. Meal and prep has three steps (cook, pack, fill the bottle), so the bag is packed by 17:00 rather than at 18:05.
- **Geniuses** comes from the course. The year goal is a specialist course for work, each month covers two modules, and each week is one module's reading and quiz. A day item is linked to Course reading on most days.

![Goals with the period ladder, the month calendar of day rings and today's goals by routine](/screenshots/guide/goals-calendar.jpg)

The month calendar shows a ring for every day the course was touched ([the Goals chapter](/guide/goals) explains the rings). Course reading runs on shift days and days off alike, and that's deliberate. A week completes once five days are met, so a goal with an item on most days can close whatever the rota does. Three good weeks complete a month, and six good months complete the course year.

## Days off and changeover days

The rota is three nights on, four off. On days off, the shift routines go unticked, which is accurate: the record shows a day off, and those routines' streaks pick up again with the next block of shifts.

The hard days are the changeovers, when your body clock is switching between nights and days and nothing on the list fits. Long-press today in the week strip and take a Skip Day. The day becomes rest, and the streak survives. You get two a week, which is exactly the number of changeover days this rota has.

## When a morning slips

Handover runs until 09:30, and the wind-down's window has closed. If you did the wind-down at 10:00, spend its 10 points from **Past** to check it in. It's still the same calendar day, the streak stays intact, and the tick counts in full. The previous day's points settled overnight, so the balance is there to spend.

A chapter read on the 23:30 break but never ticked can be ticked the next afternoon. It reads Missed, but the module week still counts it.

## First step

Build the list from the end of your shift forwards, not from midnight. Put the wind-down first, and keep its first step small enough to do with your coat still on.
