A digital nomad with clients in two time zones keeps honest windows by fixing the local routines and moving only the client ones. Working for a client in Berlin and another in New York, from a flat that's in Lisbon this month and Mexico City next, means running on three clocks: one for each client and the one your body follows. The third one usually loses.

The routines worth protecting are your own: the morning, the deep work, the walk. The client routines are the ones that move.

## Fixed routines and floating ones

From Lisbon, the day looks like this:

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 | Moves? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 07:00 | Morning | 10 | Kettle on, curtains open | No |
| 08:00 | Deep work | 25 | Phone in the drawer | No |
| 11:00 | Client A overlap | 15 | Read the overnight thread | Yes |
| 13:00 | Lunch walk | 5 | Door key in pocket | No |
| 16:00 | Client B overlap | 20 | Open their morning messages | Yes |
| 19:00 | Explore & dinner | 10 | Pick a street to eat on | No |
| 21:00 | Admin & plan | 10 | Open tomorrow's list | Sometimes |
| 22:30 | Night | 5 | Laptop closed on the desk | No |

That's 100. Deep work has the largest share, 25, because it's the part of the job nobody else will protect for you. The two client overlaps have 35 between them because those are the hours you're paid to be reachable.

The fixed routines are the easiest **Discipline** to earn. The 07:00 morning counts anywhere from 06:00 to 07:30, in Lisbon or in Mexico City, with the same steps in the same order.

## Who re-times the list?

You do. Routine times are clock times. Fly from Lisbon to Mexico City and the Berlin overlap moves from late morning to the small hours, and New York moves too. The app doesn't reshuffle anything. You decide what the new day looks like.

It takes two minutes. Open **More → Routines**, where the day is a 24-hour dial beside a timeline, tap Client A overlap, and step its start time in 10-minute increments. Do the same for Client B. Do it the evening before the flight, so the first morning in the new city already has the right shape. Leave the fixed routines alone: 07:00 is still 07:00, and getting back to it is how you get over the jet lag.

## Why widening the window backfires

It's tempting to set Client B overlap at 14:00 "so it counts whenever the call starts". But the window is only 90 minutes long. A 14:00 routine is open from 13:00 to 14:30, so it either closes before the 16:00 call or gets ticked before the call has happened. Either way, the Discipline points stop meaning anything.

Set each routine for when it really starts. If New York always runs late, put the overlap at 16:30 and leave it there. A routine that's honestly on time is worth more than one that's always fudged.

## Steps that travel between clients

- **Client A overlap:** read the overnight thread, clear blockers first, post the day's update, join the 11:30 stand-up.
- **Client B overlap:** open their morning messages, review open pull requests, join the call, note follow-ups.

Ticking those items earns **Kinetics**. Follow-ups go in with the **+** beside the chat box: type "Send the revised estimate to B tomorrow 17:00" and the date and time fill themselves in. Write "@qa-lead check the staging deploy" and the task files itself under Delegate.

## Goals that don't depend on the address

Keep year goals independent of where you are. One per client relationship works ("Ship the client A migration by Q3"), plus one of your own. Day items like "Port the export endpoint" are linked to Deep work. Ticking them earns **Geniuses**. Five good days complete a week, three good weeks a month, and six good months the year.

## Flight days and late starts

A long-haul day is a **Skip Day**: long-press today in the week strip on Home. It goes down as rest, not as a gap, and it doesn't break the streak. You get two a week.

If a routine happened but late, say deep work starting at 10:20 because the new flat's Wi-Fi took an hour to sort out, it's in **Past** by then. Before midnight, check it in from Past with points. The streak survives and the tick is a full one. An item finished on the plane and ticked on arrival still counts toward its week, even though it will show as Missed.

## Before the next move

Put a "Re-time the list" item on the day before each flight, linked to Admin & plan. Five minutes on the dial saves a week of mornings in the wrong places.
