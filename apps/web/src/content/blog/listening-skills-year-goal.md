Listening works as a year goal once you stop looking for a routine to hold it. You practise the skill inside routines you already have, with one theme a month. "Get fitter" comes with a run and "learn Spanish" with flashcards, but listening happens in conversations you can't schedule: the colleague at your desk, dinner, a call that turns serious.

## Which routines does the practice ride on?

| Time | Routine | Points | Practice that fits here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06:30 | Wake up | 5 | None |
| 07:30 | Breakfast with family | 10 | One question, then only follow-ups |
| 09:00 | Work | 40 | Summarise back before replying in a 1:1 |
| 12:30 | Lunch with a colleague | 5 | Phone stays in the bag |
| 17:30 | Commute home | 5 | None |
| 19:00 | Family time | 20 | 20 minutes of conversation, no screens |
| 21:30 | Reflect | 10 | One moment I listened well, one I didn't |
| 22:30 | Night routine | 5 | None |

Family time's 20 points say what the year is about. Reflect is the only routine created for the goal: ten minutes, added under More → Routines, whose job is to close the day.

## A theme a month

The year goal is "Become someone people feel heard by". It's vague on purpose, and the month milestones make it specific:

| Month | Theme | A day item |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Not interrupting | "In the 1:1, count two seconds before answering" |
| 2 | Body language | "Laptop closed, facing the speaker, every meeting" |
| 3 | Reflecting back | "Summarise what I heard before giving my view, twice" |
| 4 | Better questions | "Ask one 'what was that like?' at dinner" |
| 5 | Hard conversations | "Hear the whole point before defending mine" |
| 6 | Noticing my reactions | "Note when I stopped listening, and what I felt" |

Each month splits into week milestones such as "Five days of summarising back in meetings", and each week into day items linked to the routine where the practice happens.

## How do K, D and G show up for a soft skill?

- **Discipline** is being on time for the routines that carry the practice, Family time and Reflect above all. Listening needs the conversation to happen.
- **Kinetics** is working those routines' steps. Family time's are concrete: clear up, cook together, eat, phones in the drawer, one activity.
- **Geniuses** is where this goal really lives. Each ticked practice item moves a week, a month and the year, and those horizons weigh more than a loose task. Re-planning counts: if hard conversations need two months, edit month 5 and push the next theme back.

The arithmetic suits a skill that depends on other people. A week closes at 5 met days out of 7, a month at 3 met weeks, the year at 6 met months. Some Tuesdays simply have no 1:1, and five met days are enough. Late ticks count as well. If the dinner conversation happened and you only tick it at 23:00, it reads Missed, because status records timing, and it still moves the week.

## What if the practice fails in the moment?

Some days it does. You interrupt, you defend too early, you check your phone mid-sentence. Don't tick the item. Write the moment down in Reflect instead. That note is the most useful data the year produces, and you read it back before choosing next month's theme.

If Reflect itself drifts past its 22:00 close, write the note anyway and use points to check it in late from **Past** on Home that night. The streak holds. If it's late every night, move Reflect in front of the night routine rather than paying for it nightly.

## Starting this month

- Write the year goal and one month theme. Leave the rest blank.
- Attach each day item to a routine you already have.
- Add Reflect to close the day.
- At month end, read your notes before you pick the next theme.
