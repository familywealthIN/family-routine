A language learner needs two kinds of work: daily reps that only pay off through repetition, and a weekly conversation where those reps get tested against a real person. The setup below gives the reps to the routine and the conversation to the week, in five steps, for someone working toward conversational Spanish alongside a full-time job.

## Step 1: Draft the year from one prompt

Start at the top. Home → + → Goal opens the AI plan. Type the goal the way you'd say it: "Hold a 20-minute conversation in Spanish by June." It comes back with its own title and dated milestones. A typical draft runs from survival phrases and the present tense, through telling stories in the past tense, to giving opinions and handling everyday situations.

Edit it before you save. Cut anything vague, move a month if June clashes with a trip, and rename milestones in your own words. Saving creates a parent goal with the milestones under it. Treat it as draft one. Re-planning later is part of the method.

## Step 2: Put the reps into the checklist

Reps need a fixed time, so they belong in routines. These three carry the language; the other 70 of the day's 100 points go to work, exercise and the household.

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 07:10 | Language reps | 15 | Headphones in |
| 12:40 | Lunch listening | 5 | Podcast queued before lunch |
| 20:30 | Speaking practice | 10 | Open the voice recorder |

The 07:10 checklist does the heavy lifting:

1. Headphones in.
2. Review the flashcards that are due.
3. Add ten new cards.
4. Shadow one audio clip out loud.
5. Write three sentences about yesterday.

This is Kinetics at its simplest: points for doing the reps, not for planning them. Step 1 is physical on purpose. Putting headphones in is easier than "study Spanish", and once they're in, the flashcards are one tap away. If you hit a verb you want to ask your tutor about, add it as a quick task so it stays inside the slot instead of becoming another browser tab.

## Step 3: Why does the conversation belong to the week?

Because it's the test, and tests don't happen daily. Each week milestone is "One 30-minute conversation, plus the reps". Monday to Friday get small day items linked to the reps routine, such as "Reps: food and ordering". Saturday's item is the real thing: "Conversation with my exchange partner, 30 minutes."

Do the arithmetic. A week closes once five days are met, and a day is met when every item linked to that week on that date is ticked. Plan six days, Monday to Saturday. Skip Wednesday's reps and the conversation can still be the fifth met day.

## Step 4: Link every item twice

Link each day item to its routine and to that week's milestone.

![The focused routine card switched to its Week tab, showing the week goal and the day goals linked to it](/screenshots/guide/dashboard-cascade.jpg)

The Week tab on the routine card then shows this week's topic as soon as the headphones go in ([the Dashboard chapter](/guide/dashboard) covers the four tabs). Ticking a linked item also earns Geniuses, and the weight grows with the horizon. A month needs 3 met weeks and the year 6 met months, so a heavy fortnight at work doesn't end the plan. When the past tense needs six weeks instead of four, edit that month milestone halfway through. A better plan is what the G pillar pays for.

## Step 5: Decide now what happens when a morning slips

The train is late and the reps happen at 08:10. A 07:10 routine closes at 07:40, so it's now under **Past** on Home. Spend points to check it in late, today. The streak holds and the tick earns its points. Any item ticked late shows Missed, because status records timing, and it still counts toward the week.

If late reps become the pattern, change the time under More → Routines rather than paying every morning. And if the 20:30 speaking slot keeps slipping past you, switch its reminder from Notify to Alarm. It rings at exactly 20:30, and again five minutes later if the routine isn't done.

## The first week, in short

- Prompt the year, then edit the AI's plan.
- Create the reps routine with "Headphones in" as step 1.
- Book one real conversation for Saturday.
- Link every day item to its routine and its week.
