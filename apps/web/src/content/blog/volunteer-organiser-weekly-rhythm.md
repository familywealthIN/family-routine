Running a community garden on a weekly rhythm takes one daily admin slot, a week built around Saturday's workday, and @mentions that do the chasing for you. Without that, volunteer organising becomes a second job with no hours: messages arrive at lunch, the rota changes on Thursday night, and the shed key ends up in someone's other coat.

The organiser of this illustrative garden also has a full-time job, so the garden gets whatever attention is left. The fix isn't more time. It's a fixed slot, a week that builds towards Saturday, and a way of handing tasks to people that doesn't rely on remembering to chase them.

## One routine for the garden

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06:50 | Wake up | 5 | Water glass by the kettle |
| 08:30 | Day job | 35 | Inbox open at the desk |
| 12:30 | Lunch: messages | 10 | Open the volunteers' group chat |
| 18:00 | Garden admin hour | 20 | Open this week's list |
| 19:30 | Dinner | 10 | Table cleared |
| 21:00 | Plan tomorrow | 10 | Open tomorrow's list |
| 22:30 | Night | 10 | Phone charging in the kitchen |

That's 100. The admin hour's 20 is a real share. Without its own slot, garden work leaks into every other routine. The lunch slot gets 10 because that's when volunteers actually reply. Points can be changed later: tap a routine in **More → Routines** and the editor shows what's left of the 100.

## What goes on the admin checklist?

The same ground every day, which is the point:

1. Open this week's list.
2. Go through Delegate: tick what's done, nudge the rest.
3. Confirm Saturday's rota.
4. One job for the plot: order seed, book the skip, chase the water company.
5. Post one update to the group.

Each ticked item earns **Kinetics**. Anything that comes up halfway through goes in with the **+** beside the chat box on Home. Type "Ring the council about the compost bins tomorrow at 10am" and the day and time fill themselves in. The task lands inside the admin hour, so garden work stays in the garden's slot. Starting that hour between 17:00 and 18:30 earns **Discipline**.

## Handing tasks to volunteers

Most organising is asking other people to do things and then checking that they did. When a task @mentions someone, it goes into **Delegate** on Priority: "@rota-lead swap the Saturday shifts", "@shed-keeper confirm the seed order arrived".

![The four Priority quadrants, Do now, Plan, Delegate and Automate, with the day's goals grouped by routine](/screenshots/guide/priority-quadrants.jpg)

Priority lays the day out in four parts ([the Priority chapter](/guide/priority) covers the sorting rules):

- **Do now:** what you started today. Keep it to three or fewer.
- **Plan:** anything dated later, such as next month's open day.
- **Delegate:** everything handed over that you still need to see finished.
- **Automate:** tasks started with an agent.

Delegate is the chasing list, and item 2 of the checklist works through it every evening.

## A week that builds to Saturday

The garden runs on a weekly cycle, so the plan is a week goal: **"Saturday workday ready"**, with one item for each weekday, all linked to the admin hour:

- Monday: confirm who's coming.
- Tuesday: decide the jobs (beds, paths, compost).
- Wednesday: check tools, order what's missing.
- Thursday: post the final rota.
- Friday: check the weather and post the Plan B.

Five dates, one item each. The week completes once all five days are met, so a full Monday-to-Friday run does it. Above it, "Spring planting done" completes after three good weeks, and the year goal, "Two open days and a waiting list for plots", after six good months. Ticking those linked items earns **Geniuses**.

## When the day job derails the admin hour

Some evenings the day job wins. If you did the admin hour but at 20:30, it will be in **Past** because its window ended at 18:30. Check it in from there before midnight, paying with points, and the run of days holds. A Wednesday item done on Wednesday but ticked on Thursday still counts toward the week, even though it will show **Missed**.

If the Saturday workday takes over the whole day, long-press today in the week strip that Saturday and choose **Skip Day**. You get two a week, the streak stays, and the week goal doesn't need Saturday: the five weekdays already complete it.

## The next step

Set up the admin hour with its five steps. Then, tonight, take one task you've been carrying around in your head and move it onto the Delegate list with an @mention. Once it's written down, you don't have to keep remembering it.
