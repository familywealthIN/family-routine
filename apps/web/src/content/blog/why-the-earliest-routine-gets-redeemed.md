Your earliest routine is the one you keep checking in late because nobody ticks an app at 05:30 with their hands full, not because getting up failed. For many early risers, the routine that lands in **Past** most often isn't the hard workout or the long work block. It's the five-point "Wake up" that nobody would call difficult.

If your Discipline score has plateaued and the same early routine keeps showing up in Past, here's how to find out why and what to change.

## Step 1: confirm the pattern

Don't go on how it feels. Open **More → Progress**, choose Month, and look at *On the clock*. It splits check-ins into on time and late for each routine. A typical early-riser plateau looks like this:

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 | Usually |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 05:30 | Wake up | 5 | Fill a glass of water | Checked in late from Past |
| 06:00 | Reading | 5 | Phone face-down in the hall | On time |
| 07:00 | Yoga | 10 | Unroll the mat | On time |
| 07:20 | Run | 10 | Lace the running shoes | On time |
| 08:00 | Plan the day | 5 | Open today's list | Mixed |
| 08:30 | Deep work | 30 | Clear chats and reviews | On time |

The other 35 of the 100 points sit in the evening routines. The pattern is clear: the earliest routine is the problem and everything after it is fine. So getting up isn't the issue. If it were, yoga at 07:00 would be late too. The issue is the tick.

## Step 2: name the actual failure

Three causes are common. See which matches your mornings.

**A. You did it, but didn't tick it.** At 05:30 your hands are full: water, kettle, bathroom. The phone is charging across the room. By the time you pick it up, the window, which runs from 04:30 to 06:00, has closed. This is the most frequent case.

**B. The reminder doesn't reach you.** The routine is on Notify, which sends a reminder around the time you usually do it. Half-awake, you swipe it away.

**C. The time is aspirational.** You chose 05:30 because it sounded disciplined. In practice you're up around 06:10 most days, ten minutes after the window has closed.

## Why does each cause need a different fix?

Because each one breaks a different link between waking up and the tick.

**For A: put the tick inside the action.** Change step 1 to something done with the phone in your hand: "Pick up the phone from the charger and tick Wake up." It sounds silly, but it ties the tick to the first physical thing you do. Moving the charger next to the kettle does the same job.

**For B: switch to Alarm.** In **More → Routines**, tap Wake up and change the reminder from Notify to Alarm. It rings at exactly the start time, then again five minutes later if the routine still isn't ticked. The second ring is the one that gets you.

**For C: move the time.** Change the start to 06:10 and push Reading to 06:30. Start times move in 10-minute steps, and the 24-hour dial on the same screen shows whether the morning still fits. An honest 06:10 beats an aspirational 05:30. Changing the plan to match your life is the "improve the plan" step of the loop, the Geniuses part.

After a fix, Home should show Wake up ticked and Reading due at the top of the morning ([the Dashboard chapter](/guide/dashboard) covers the card).

## What a late check-in is for

None of this makes a late check-in wrong. If you did get up and the window simply closed, check the routine in from Past that day with points. The streak holds, agents attached to the routine still run, and the tick earns its points like any other. Points are added overnight, so a strong evening can cover the morning.

But if you redeem the same routine every morning, the points are covering for a schedule that doesn't work. Each late check-in costs that routine's points, and those are points you then can't spend on a day that really went wrong.

## Step 3: re-test for a week

Make one change, not three, so you know which one worked. Run it for seven mornings, then check *On the clock* again. If Wake up has moved from late to on time, you're done. If not, try the next fix.

Also check when Good morning arrives, under **More → Notifications**. If it comes at 06:30, after the window has closed, it only tells you what you've already missed. Move it inside the window.

## Why a five-point routine deserves this

The first tick sets the tone for the morning. When Wake up goes in on time, every routine after it starts from a win, and the run toward the 10K goal ("Move before 08:00 on five days") keeps getting met. A late check-in at 08:10 means the morning is spent catching up. That's worth a week of attention, even for five points.
