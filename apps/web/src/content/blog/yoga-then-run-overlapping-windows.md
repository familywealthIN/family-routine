Yoga followed by a run works best as two routines twenty minutes apart, each with its own cue and its own tick. The obvious setup is a single "Yoga and run" routine, and it's usually the wrong one. Here are the questions that come up when you split them, in the order people tend to ask.

## Why two routines and not one?

Because they fail separately. Some mornings the yoga happens and the run doesn't: it's raining, there isn't time, or your legs say no. A combined routine leaves you two bad options: tick it and record a run that never happened, or leave it and lose a yoga session that did. Two routines record both honestly.

They also need different cues. Yoga starts when the mat is unrolled. The run starts when your shoes go on. Each routine has one first step, so the two cues need two routines.

| Time | Routine | Points | Step 1 (the cue) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 06:00 | Wake up | 5 | Glass of water |
| 06:30 | Yoga | 15 | Unroll the mat |
| 06:50 | Run | 15 | Shoes on at the door |
| 07:40 | Breakfast | 10 | Kettle on |
| 08:30 | Work | 40 | Open today's list |
| 19:00 | Evening walk | 5 | Coat on |
| 22:00 | Night routine | 10 | Mat back by the bed |

The day adds up to 100. The last step of the night routine puts the mat by the bed, so the evening sets out the first cue of the morning.

## How do the two windows overlap?

Each window opens an hour before its start time and closes half an hour after:

- **Yoga, 06:30:** 05:30 to 07:00.
- **Run, 06:50:** 05:50 to 07:20.

They overlap for 70 minutes. Finish twenty minutes of yoga at 06:50, tick it, put your shoes on, and the run's window has been open for an hour. One tick leads into the next, and both earn Discipline.

## Which one do I tick first?

Tick each one when you finish it: yoga as you roll up the mat, the run when you're back through the door. With the overlap, getting the order wrong costs nothing. Ticking as you go keeps the record straight, and the routine chat posts its after-tick message at the right moment.

Inside each routine, work through the checklist. Yoga: mat, sun salutations, standing sequence, five minutes still, mat away. Run: shoes, warm-up walk, the run, the walk back. Each line you tick earns Kinetics.

## What if only one happens?

Tick the one that did. If you swapped the morning run for a lunchtime one, the Run routine is waiting in **Past** on Home from 07:20 onwards. Tick it from there that afternoon and it costs the routine's 15 points. The streak keeps going.

![A past day opened from the week strip, with today's list and its D, K and G badges, that day's checklist and a chat about the day](/screenshots/guide/dashboard-past.jpg)

Tap yesterday in the week strip to see which of the two happened. Past days can be reviewed but not ticked any more ([the Dashboard chapter](/guide/dashboard)).

If the run didn't happen at all, leave it blank. A week of five yoga sessions and three runs is useful. A week that claims seven of each isn't.

## How do the goals tell them apart?

Each routine feeds its own goal. Yoga could sit under "Hold a 20-minute practice without breaks" and the run under "Run a 10K in the autumn". Give each a week goal with five dated items linked to its own routine. A week where the yoga is solid and the running patchy will show you exactly that: one week closed, the other at three met days out of five. Linked items earn Geniuses on top.

## What if the run keeps slipping?

It's usually the run that goes first, two or three mornings in a row, while the yoga carries on. Open **More → Progress** and read *On the clock*, which compares on-time and late check-ins for each routine. If the run is often late, it doesn't need more willpower. Move it to 07:00 in **More → Routines** and give the transition some room. If it's often missing altogether, a short yoga session first thing and an evening run might simply suit you better.
